## Intro
- Article 368 empowers the Parliament to amend the Constitution of India by way of addition, variation or repeal.
- While the Parliament can ammend any part of the Constitution, the amending power is not absolute, it cannot change the fundamental principles like Democracy, Secularism, Judicial Review, which are called Basic Str of the Constitution.
- Basic Structure doctrine is a judicial innovation emphasizing Parliament's amending power is not absolute; has some "implied limitations". The Parliament cannot use Article-368 to destroy, alter or abrograte those core principles is called "Basic Structure" eg: Judicial Review
- The system ensures while the Constitution remains flexible enough to adapt itself with changing societal needs, its democratic secular soul does not get hijacked by transient political majority.

1. Germany: written BS in the constitution

## Advantages/Benefits
1. Prevents Totalitarianism balancing amending power under A-368 with limited amendment.
2. Protects Constitutional supremacy
3. Protects Democratic Institutions like ECI, Judiciary, Federal str
4. Evolving nature
5. Global Jurisprudential export- India's greatest contribution is BS
6. Protects Minorities

## Disadvantages/De-Merits/Challenges
1. Tyranny of Unelected over elected representatives of 1.5B people
2. **judicial solipsism**—where the constitutionality of a law depends on the judicial philosophy of individual benches.
3. Internal inconsistency with 3:2 or 4:1 decisions signifies not all judges have similar interpretations.
4. Highly subjective and vague without any defined boundary
5. Policy paralysis: Accountable Parliament fails to reform critical democratic institutions. eg: Judicial appointments
6. Judicial inconsistency: Called quickly in judicial matters(suo moto in NJAC) vs called fewer in number in other matters(Governors hagemony in SLA bills)
7. Extra constitutional origin, not mentioned in any of 395 articles - difficulty in acceptence
8. Judiciary becomes super-constituent body dictating what Parliament cannot amend.

## Conclusion
