## BRICS

### Evolution
1. Post USSR collapse: threat of US-Unipolar world
2. Western dominated IMF, World Bank, etc
3. RIC - emergence: India and China were emerging countries.
4. RIC - Russian PM Yevgeny Primakov gave the concept of Russia-India-China: strategic triangle
5. RIC: Foreign Ministers began meeting informally
6. IBSA Dialogue Forum 2003: Ind-Brazil and South Africa - large democracies from 3 continent
7. Despite rapid growth - IMF and World Bank had low voting powers for these emerging countries
8. 1st BRICS summit: Spurred by 2008 financial crisis, June 2009(1st BRICS summit: Yekaterinburg, Russia): Agenda- Reform Global financial architecture
9. 2010-11: South Africa entry: Sanya, China 2011
10. 4th Summit 2012: Discussion to create multilateral banks to finance infra in emerging economies
11. 6th Summit(Fortaleza, Brazil, 2014): Fortaleza Declaration
    1.  NEW DEVELOPMENT BANK(NDB)
        1.  HQ: Shanghai, China - Non western alternative to world bank - equal voting rights and no veto power
        2.  Contingent Reserve Arrangement(CRA): $100B - to protect member state against short term BoP crisis and global liquidity shocks - (alternative to IMF conditional bailouts)
12. 7th Summit(Ufa, Russia, 2015): long term trade, investment and supply chain frameworks on 3 pillars
    1.  Political and security - counter terrorism, settle regional conflict
    2.  Economic and Financial - local currency settlement, NDB lending
    3.  Culture and People to people connect: Academic, parliamentary and sports exchange
13. 15th summit: Johhanesburg II Declaration - expansion
14. 2024: Kazan Summit
    1.  New Members: 
        1.  Egypt
        2.  Ethiopia
        3.  Iran
        4.  UAE
        5.  Soudi Arabia - invited member
    2.  Partner Country category - 30 countries
15. 2025 - Indonesia joined + Malasia, Thiland, Belarus, Bolivia, Cuba,Kazakhastan, Nigeria, Uganda, Uzbekistan and Vietnam - Partner countries
16. Key Agenda
    1.  Cross border local currency payment mechanism
    2.  Global South Coalition - Primary Non-Western platform
17. Weitage: 
    1.  37% Global GDP
    2.  45% population
