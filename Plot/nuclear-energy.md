### Nuclear Development of India
1. Dr. Homi J. Bhabha laid foundation in 1948.
2. 1948 - Atomic Energy Commission
3. 1954 - Department of Atomic Energy
4. 1956 - commissioned Apsara - Asia's first research reactor at Trombay.
5. Dr. Bhabha formulated 3 STAGE PLAN
   1. STAGE-1: PHWR: U-238 -> electricity + Pu-239
   2. STAGE-2: FBR: Pu-239 + U => elec + Fissile U-233 from Thorium-232
   3. STAGE-3: Thorium Based reactors (Advanced Heavy Water Reactors - AHWRs): Th-232/U-233 -> Sustainable energy generation for centuries
6. 1969 - TAPS: Tarapur Atomic Power Station comissioned using US imported BWR
7. 1974 - Smiling Buddha: India's peaceful nuclear test.
8. 1974-1990 - Global sanctions and isolation. Indian scienties indeginised reactor technology.
9. 2008 - Civil Nuclear Agreement: Indo-US Nuclear Agreement. India got access to uranium supplies and nuclear technology.
10. 2026
    1.  Capacity: 8780 MW - DAE [3.1% of India's elec generation]
    2.  24 reactors across 7 power stations
    3.  Tarapur-Mh, Rawatbhata-Raj, Kalpakkam & Kundankulam - Tn, Narora-UP, Kakrapar-Guj, Kaiga-Kar
    4.  Target @2047: 100,000 MW
