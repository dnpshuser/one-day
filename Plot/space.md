### India's space journey

1. 1962: INCOSPAR (Indian National Committee for Space Research) estb by Dr. Vikram Sarabhai and Dr. Homi J. Bhabha.
2. 1963 Nov 21: launched Nike-Apache sounding rocket from the Thumba Equatorial Rocket Launching Station (TERLS) in Kerala.
3. 1969: ISRO replaced INCOSPAR.
4. 1975: first indigenous satellite: ARYABHATA with a Soviat launch vehicle.
5. 1980: SLV-3(Satellite Launch Vehicle) placed Rohini RS-1 satellite into orbit - India became 7th nation with satellite launch capabil.
6. 1990 - PSLV started
7. GSLV developed
8. 2017 - PSLV launched 104 satellites in a single mission[Mission Name: *PSLV-C37 / Cartosat-2 Series Satellite Mission*]
9. Constellations: 
   1.  INSAT/GSAT Series: Telecommunication + meteorology
   2.  IRS Series: Remote sensing
   3.  NavIC (IRNSS): Regional Satellite Navigation System
10. Key Events
    1.  Chandrayan-1: 2008 - Water on Moon
    2.  Mangalyaan: 2014 - 1st attempt Mars mission successful
    3.  Chandrayaan-2: 2019 - High resolution image(upto 30cm) on lunar surface.
    4.  Chandrayaan-3: successful soft landing near lunar South Pole(Aug23 - designated as National Space Day)
    5.  Aditya-L1: 2023-24 - 1st solar mission at L1
    6.  SPADEX: 2025: space docking successful-India just the 4th nation after US, Russia and China.
