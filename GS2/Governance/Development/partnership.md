### Partnership
1. Intro
Public-private and multi-stakeholder partnerships are vital for mobilising capital, innovation, and execution capacity in a resource-constrained development state.

2. Importance
- Partnerships bridge gaps in financing, technology, and project management.
- They improve delivery of infrastructure, health, sanitation, and urban services.
- They enable risk-sharing between the state and private enterprise.
- Local communities, NGOs, and CSOs can contribute social accountability and local knowledge.
- They are essential to meeting developmental targets in a growing economy.

3. Instances in Indian development process
- PPP models in highways, airports, metro rail, and power infrastructure have expanded connectivity and efficiency.
- Corporate social responsibility has supported education, health, and community development initiatives.
- State-community partnerships in watershed management and rural livelihoods have improved local resilience.
- Digital and e-governance pilots often rely on public-private cooperation for scale and innovation.
- Partnerships must be governed through transparency, equity, and public interest safeguards.

4. Conclusion
Development partnerships can accelerate inclusive growth when aligned with social justice, environmental sustainability, and democratic accountability.
