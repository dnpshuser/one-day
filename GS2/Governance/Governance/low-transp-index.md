### Integrity Index of Transparency International: Low Rank
1. Intro
A low rank in Transparency International’s Corruption Perception Index reflects weak institutional integrity, poor accountability, and structural governance challenges in public systems.

2. Causes and factors
  1. Legal
  - Weak enforcement, fragmented laws, and slow adjudication limit deterrence against corruption.
  2. Political
  - Political patronage, opaque funding, and transactional politics weaken institutional integrity.
  3. Economic
  - Informal economies, rent-seeking, and procurement opacity increase opportunities for corruption.
  4. Social
  - Social inequality and low civic awareness reduce pressure for accountability.
  5. Cultural
  - Tolerance of bureaucratic discretion and weak ethical standards erode public trust.

3. How to improve rank
- Strengthen institutional autonomy, digital transparency, and audit mechanisms.
- Improve procurement integrity, asset disclosure, and enforcement of anti-corruption laws.
- Expand citizen participation and social audit for public services and welfare schemes.
- Build ethical public administration through values-based training and accountability systems.
- Promote transparency in political funding and administrative decision-making.

4. Committee reports
- Governance reform reports and parliamentary committees stress the need for better integrity, fair law enforcement, and institutional capacity.
- They highlight the importance of reducing discretion and improving trust in public institutions.

5. Conclusion
Improving integrity demands constitutional governance, rule of law, and ethical public administration, essential for sustainable development and credible democratic institutions.
