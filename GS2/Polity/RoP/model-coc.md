
### Model Code of Conduct
1. Fair election conduct and political ethics
2. Evolution and scope
3. ECI enforcement role
4. Challenges and compliance
5. Reform measures
6. Conclusion

---

1. Intro
The Model Code of Conduct is a voluntary framework that regulates political conduct during elections and preserves the fairness of the democratic process. 

2. Evolution
- It emerged through electoral practice and institutional guidance, formalised by the Election Commission.
- It ensures that campaigning remains free, fair, and non-abusive.

3. ECI role in enforcement
- The Election Commission monitors violations, issues directives, and ensures political parties act within constitutional and ethical limits.
- Its role is central in maintaining electoral fairness.

4. Challenges
- Political parties often treat the code as a soft framework, especially during highly polarised campaigns.
- Enforcement remains difficult without stronger legal penalties.

5. Solutions - committee recommendations
- Stronger enforcement, clear violation categories, and enhanced digital monitoring can improve compliance.
- Greater transparency in campaign finance and political advertising is also necessary.

6. Conclusion
A robust electoral code protects democratic fairness, strengthens voter trust, and advances SDG 16 through accountable political behaviour.
