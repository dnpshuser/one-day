### Electoral Reforms
1. Democratic quality and institutional trust
2. Need for reform and One Nation One Election
3. EVM and electoral integrity
4. ECI reforms and oversight
5. Federalism and governance implications
6. Conclusion

---

1. Intro
Electoral reforms are essential to strengthen transparency, trust, and democratic quality in a large and diverse democracy like India. 

2. Need - ref: "One Nation One Election"
- Synchronising elections can improve governance continuity, reduce cost, and prevent election fatigue.
- However, it must be balanced with federalism, administrative capacity, and constitutional viability.

3. EVM - controversy
   1. Challenges before ECI - hold trustworthiness
   - Trust in electronic voting requires transparency, auditability, and public confidence.
   - ECI must maintain procedural integrity and open scrutiny to preserve legitimacy.

4. ECI - suggested reforms of 2016 [asked in 2017]
   1. importance/significance in holding democracy
   - Electoral roll revision, stricter disclosure norms, and improved enforcement enhance democratic accountability.

5. One Nation One Election
   1. Pros and Cons
   - Pros: reduced cost, continuity of governance, better resource utilisation.
   - Cons: federalism concerns, legal complexity, and possible misuse of state-level political narratives.

6. Conclusion
Electoral reform should deepen democratic trust without undermining federalism, ensuring inclusive and credible governance for the future.
