### Emergency
1. Extraordinary powers and federal balance
2. Grounds and constitutional basis
3. Judicial oversight and limits
4. Financial emergency and federal impact
5. Democratic safeguards
6. Conclusion

---

1. Intro
Emergency provisions are constitutional safety valves designed for extraordinary situations, but their use must remain limited to preserve federalism and democratic legitimacy. 

2. Factors for reduced frequency of A-356
   1. legal
   - Judicial review and constitutional safeguards restrict arbitrary dismissal of state governments.
   1. political
   - Federal coalition politics and institutional caution have reduced misuse of Article 356.

3. Financial Emergency
   1. circumstances
   - It can be declared when financial stability of the Union is threatened.
   2. consequences
   - It allows central control over finances and can affect federal autonomy.

4. Conclusion
Emergency powers must remain exceptional, because constitutional democracy is strongest when the rule of law constrains extraordinary authority.
