### Essence of Rights
1. Intro
2. what does it mean ?
3. how established ?
4. significance
5. Conclusion

---

1. Intro: The "Essence of the Rights" doctrine is a judicial filter formulated by the 9-judge Constitution Bench in I.R. Coelho (2007) to bridge the gap between individual Fundamental Rights (Part III) and the abstract Basic Structure doctrine.
2. what does it mean ?
   1. Not all laws violating FR changes Basic Str
   2. Introduced 2 tier check on laws of 9th Schedule
   3. 9th Schedule Act -> 
      1. (The Infringement Check)No violation of FR: Act is valid
      2. Violation of FR - "Essence of Rights" Test
         1. Doesn't Destroys ESSENCE, CORE VALUE, or FOUNDATIONAL IDENTITY of that Fundamental Right? -> A-31(B saves it)
         2. Destroes core identity(14,19,21) -> Act is struck down
   4. Articles 14, 19, and 21 forms the Golden Triangle which is a part of the Basic Structure.
   5. To determine whether the "essence" of a right is violated, I.R. Coelho imported the "Effect Doctrine" from the R.C. Cooper (1970) and Maneka Gandhi (1978) cases.
      1. consequence and operational effect is seen on the citizen's life, not merely the formal legislative intent or label given by Parliament.
      2. If the real-world consequence of the law effectively nullifies the protections of the Golden Triangle, it breaches the Basic Structure.
3. how established ?
   1. est through IR Coelho 2007 case by a 9-bench judgement.
4. significance
   1. corrected the loopholes left in earlier cases: MM 1980
   2. Prevented the judicial review immunity for all laws which were being exploited. TN 69% reservation in 9th schedule
   3. Protected FR from being exploited by 9th schedules.
5. Conclusion: With Essence of Rights establishment, the SC upheld the principles of FR protecting it from 9th schedule loopholes.
