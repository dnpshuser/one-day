### MP
1. Representation and parliamentary responsibility
2. Role of legislators in democracy
3. Declining individual voice and debate quality
4. Anti-defection and accountability issues
5. Reforms for stronger representation
6. Conclusion

---

1. Intro
Members of Parliament are the primary representatives of the people, and their quality of participation shapes the health of parliamentary democracy. 

2. Declining individual role and quality debate
   1. data for recent years
   - Parliamentary productivity has often fallen sharply in recent years due to disruptions and time loss.
   - Question Hour and legislative discussion are frequently compromised, reducing accountability.
   2. Reasons
      - Increasing party discipline, electoral strategy, and poor institutional culture reduce independent legislative voice.
      - Anti defection law, though necessary, may also discourage dissent and reduce individual accountability.

3. Conclusion
A vibrant democracy needs MPs who can deliberate, dissent, and represent citizens meaningfully; this is essential for SDG 16 and democratic legitimacy.
