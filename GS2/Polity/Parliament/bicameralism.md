### Bicameralism
1. Parliamentary structure and second chamber role
2. Rajya Sabha and federal representation
3. State legislative councils and asymmetry
4. Joint sessions and legislative coordination
5. Deliberation and accountability
6. Conclusion

---

1. Intro
Bicameralism balances territorial representation with population-based representation, strengthening deliberation in a large and diverse democracy. 

2. Rajya Sabha
   1. transformation: useless stepney tyre -> useful supporting organ
   - Rajya Sabha has evolved from being seen as a nominal second chamber to a significant forum for federal representation and policy review.
   - It brings expertise, state interests, and legislative scrutiny.
      1. factors
      - Federal design, legislative scrutiny, and policy expertise have strengthened its role.
      2. occurrences - areas of impact
      - It is particularly important in finance, federal issues, and constitutional scrutiny.

3. State Legislative Councils
   1. Constitutional provisions
   - Article 169 allows states to create Legislative Councils.
   2. working status
   - Most councils remain limited in practical utility, often due to political reluctance and low functional relevance.

4. Joint session
   1. constitutional provisions and conditions
   - Article 108 provides for a joint sitting in case of disagreement between Houses.
   - It is rarely used but ensures legislative completion in a democracy.

5. conclusion
Bicameralism improves democratic quality by ensuring both representation and deliberation, supporting good governance and constitutional harmony.
