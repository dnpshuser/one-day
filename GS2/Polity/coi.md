# Constitution of India: Article, Clause, Amendment and Procedure Map

> **How to use this**: read the first column as the article's core rule, then the second as its MCQ hooks: exceptions, thresholds, institutional distinctions and limits. “Procedure” is only one type of hook. “Amendment” lists the most important changes, not every amendment that merely altered a schedule or phrase.

## Part I: Union and its Territory (Articles 1-4)

| Article | Provision and clause cue | Prerequisite / procedure | Important amendment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | India, that is Bharat, is a Union of States; territory includes States, UTs and acquired territory. | None. | 7th Amendment: reorganisation and States/UT framework. |
| 2 | Admission or establishment of new States. | Parliament by law; terms may be prescribed. | 36th: Sikkim became a State. |
| 3 | Formation, alteration of area, boundaries or names of States. | **President's recommendation**; Bill referred to affected State Legislature for views (views are not binding). | 5th, 6th, 18th, 36th, 53rd, 55th, 56th, 69th, 100th. |
| 4 | Laws under Articles 2-3 may amend First/Fourth Schedules and are not Article 368 amendments. | Ordinary parliamentary law; consequential provisions permitted. | 7th and State reorganisation amendments. |

## Dense-range MCQ hooks

These are the individual distinctions most likely to be lost when articles are read as a range:

- **Articles 89-98**: 89 Chairman/Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha; 90 vacation/removal of Deputy Chairman; 91 acting Chairman; 92 Chairman/Deputy Chairman cannot preside during their removal motion; 93 Speaker/Deputy Speaker of Lok Sabha; 94 vacation/removal; 95 acting Speaker; 96 Speaker/Deputy Speaker cannot preside during removal motion; 97 salaries/allowances; 98 separate secretariats of Parliament Houses.
- **Articles 101-104**: 101 vacation of seats (dual membership, disqualification, resignation); 102 parliamentary disqualifications (office of profit, unsound mind, insolvency, foreign citizenship, statutory disqualification); 103 President decides MP disqualification **after Election Commission opinion**; 104 penalty for sitting/voting before oath or while disqualified.
- **Articles 105-106**: 105 privilege belongs to Parliament and its committees; freedom of speech is subject to the Constitution and House rules, and does not create immunity from proceedings outside the House for unrelated conduct; 106 salaries/allowances are fixed by Parliament.

## Procedure traps to remember

- **Article 3**: President's recommendation is mandatory; affected State's view is sought but is not binding.
- **Money Bill**: President's recommendation before introduction; Speaker's certification; Rajya Sabha has 14 days; no joint sitting.
- **Constitutional amendment**: special majority; no President's discretion after passage; half-State ratification for federal provisions; no joint sitting.
- **Emergency**: Cabinet's written advice for Article 352; parliamentary approval within one month for Articles 352 and 356 differs; Article 360 approval within two months.
- **President/VP removal**: distinct from ordinary no-confidence; notice, investigation/debate and special majorities are constitutionally prescribed.
- **Schedules**: Ninth Schedule is not an absolute immunity after *I.R. Coelho*; Tenth Schedule decisions remain subject to judicial review after *Kihoto Hollohan*.
