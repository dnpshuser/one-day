### Rani Avanti Bai Lodhi
1. Born: 16th Aug 1831 in MP
2. married to Vikramaditya Singh of Ramgarh Priencely State
3. Ran administration when her husband went ill
4. Court of Wards (कोर्ट ऑफ वार्ड्स): British authorities brought Ramgarh under this system, declaring the king incapable of ruling (शासन में अक्षम घोषित) and his heirs minors (उत्तराधिकारी अवयस्क)
5. The Court of Wards allowed the Company to take over the administration of an estate (रियासत का प्रशासन) whose ruler it deemed unfit — a favoured instrument of annexation (विलय).
6. Rani Avanti Bai opposed - mobilised farmers - call for resistance
7. Battle of Kheri, 1857: 23rd Nov, 1857 - defeated British army led by Mandla Deputy Commissioner Waddington
8. March 1858: British major attach and surrounded - She chose ***self-sacrifice rather than surrender***
9. Symbol of courage and Patriotism
10. Recognition: Rani Avanti Bai Lodhi Women’s Battalion of the UP Provincial Armed Constabulary (PAC) at Badaun (बदायूँ).
11. 1857 Women Leaders:
