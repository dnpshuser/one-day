British PM Ramsay MacDonald's Communal Award (सांप्रदायिक पंचाट), August
1932, gave separate electorates to the Depressed Classes.
Gandhi began a fast unto death in Yerwada Jail (September 1932) against
this.
It ended with the Poona Pact (पूना समझौता) with Dr. B.R. Ambedkar: joint
electorates with reserved seats.


On 8 August 1942, the All India Congress Committee (AICC) adopted the
Quit India Resolution (भारत छोड़ो प्रस्ताव) at Gowalia Tank, Bombay, Gandhi
gave the call "Do or Die" (करो या मरो).
Congress ministries resigned in 1939; the Cripps Mission came in March
1942; the Executive Council was expanded in 1941.
