### Interim Government of India: 2 Sept, 1946
1. Royal Indian Navy Mutiny, 1946 + World War 2 acclerated Transfer of power
2. Cabinet Mission Plan 1946, March
   1. Members
      1. Pethick Lawrence(Sec of State for India)
      2. Stafford Cripps (President of Board of Trade)
      3. A.V. Alexamder (First lord of the Admiralty)
   2. Rejected demand for Pakistan
   3. Proposed 3 tier federal structure
      1. Union
      2. Section
      3. Provincial
         1. Sec A - Hindu Majority
         2. Sec B - Muslim Majority
         3. Sec C - Bengal and Assam
   4. provided 389 member constituent assembly
   5. formation of interim government 
3. purpose: 
   1. form cabinet govt, 
   2. facilitate transfer of power 
4. Legal Framework: func under Govt of India Act, 1919 through restructured viceroy executive council
5. Interim Govt Members
   1. Viceroy: Wavel
   2. Vice President: Nehru - de facto PM and head of internal administration
6. Direct Action Day: 16 Aug, 1946
7. Muslim League entry on Interim Govt: 26th Oct, 1946 without withdrawing its rejection of Cabinet Mission Plan
