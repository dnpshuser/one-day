| Amendment Act | Year | Key Provisions & Important Changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1st Amendment** | 1951 | Added **Ninth Schedule** to protect land reform laws from judicial review; inserted **Articles 31A and 31B**; added reasonable restrictions on freedom of speech under Art 19(2) ("public order", "friendly relations with foreign states", "incitement to an offence"); enabled special provisions for advancement of socially and educationally backward classes under Art 15(4). |
| **2nd Amendment** | 1952 | Readjusted representation scale in Lok Sabha by removing the upper ceiling of 750,000 population per member under Article 81(1)(b). |
| **3rd Amendment** | 1954 | Re-enacted **Entry 33 of Concurrent List (Seventh Schedule)** to empower Parliament to regulate production, supply, and distribution of essential commodities (foodstuffs, cattle fodder, raw cotton, jute). |
| **4th Amendment** | 1955 | Made adequacy of compensation for compulsory acquisition of private property non-justiciable under Article 31(2); expanded Article 31A; added more acts to Ninth Schedule. |
| **5th Amendment** | 1955 | Empowered the President to prescribe a time limit for State Legislatures to convey views on Central laws affecting state areas, boundaries, or names under Article 3. |
| **6th Amendment** | 1956 | Added taxes on sale/purchase of goods in inter-state trade to the Union List (Entry 92A) and amended Articles 269 and 286. |
| **7th Amendment** | 1956 | Implemented **States Reorganisation Act, 1956**; abolished classification of states into Parts A, B, C, and D; established **14 States and 6 Union Territories**; provided for common High Courts for two or more states; introduced Articles 350A and 350B for linguistic minorities. |
| **8th Amendment** | 1960 | Extended reservation of seats for SCs, STs, and Anglo-Indians in Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies for an additional 10 years (until 1970) under Article 334. |
| **9th Amendment** | 1960 | Facilitated transfer of Indian territory of **Berubari Union (West Bengal)** to Pakistan pursuant to the 1958 Indo-Pakistan border agreement (consequent to the *In re Berubari Union* advisory opinion). |
| **10th Amendment** | 1961 | Formally incorporated **Dadra and Nagar Haveli** into the Indian Union as a Union Territory following liberation from Portuguese rule. |
| **11th Amendment** | 1961 | Changed election procedure of Vice President from a joint sitting of Parliament to an **electoral college**; barred challenge to presidential/vice-presidential elections on grounds of vacancies in the electoral college. |
| **12th Amendment** | 1961 | Formally incorporated **Goa, Daman, and Diu** into the Indian Union as a Union Territory after liberation from Portuguese rule. |
| **13th Amendment** | 1962 | Conferred statehood on **Nagaland** and inserted **Article 371A** granting special protections regarding Naga customary law, land, and resources. |
| **14th Amendment** | 1962 | Incorporated **Puducherry** into the Indian Union; created legislative assemblies and councils of ministers for certain Union Territories under Article 239A. |
| **15th Amendment** | 1963 | Raised the **retirement age of High Court judges from 60 to 62 years**; enabled High Courts to issue writs beyond territorial jurisdiction if cause of action arose within their territory. |
| **16th Amendment** | 1963 | Empowered the State to impose restrictions on freedom of speech, assembly, and association in the interests of the **"sovereignty and integrity of India"**; amended third-schedule oaths. |
| **17th Amendment** | 1964 | Prohibited acquisition of land under personal cultivation within statutory ceiling limits without market-value compensation; added 44 land reform acts to Ninth Schedule. |
| **18th Amendment** | 1966 | Clarified Article 3 so that the word "State" includes "Union Territories" for the purpose of reorganizing boundaries (facilitated creation of Punjab, Haryana, and UT of Chandigarh). |
| **19th Amendment** | 1966 | Abolished Election Tribunals and vested the jurisdiction to try election disputes directly in the **High Courts**. |
| **20th Amendment** | 1966 | Inserted **Article 233A** to validate appointments and judgments of certain district judges in Uttar Pradesh that had been struck down by courts. |
| **21st Amendment** | 1967 | Added **Sindhi** as the 15th constitutionally recognized language in the **Eighth Schedule**. |
| **22nd Amendment** | 1969 | Inserted **Article 244A** enabling Parliament to establish an autonomous sub-state within Assam (paved the way for Meghalaya). |
| **23rd Amendment** | 1969 | Extended SC/ST and Anglo-Indian reservations in Lok Sabha and Assemblies for another 10 years (until 1980); discontinued ST reservations in Nagaland. |
| **24th Amendment** | 1971 | Enacted to nullify the *Golaknath* judgment; affirmed Parliament's constituent power to amend any part of the Constitution including Fundamental Rights under Article 368; made Presidential assent to constitutional amendment bills mandatory. |
| **25th Amendment** | 1971 | Curtailed property compensation rights by replacing the word "compensation" with "amount" under Article 31; inserted **Article 31C** giving primacy to DPSPs under Art 39(b) and 39(c) over Articles 14, 19, and 31. |
| **26th Amendment** | 1971 | **Abolished Privy Purses** and official recognition of the titles/privileges of the former rulers of princely states (omitted Articles 291 and 362; inserted Article 363A). |
| **27th Amendment** | 1971 | Reorganized northeastern areas; provided for a legislature and council of ministers for Arunachal Pradesh and Mizoram; empowered administrators of UTs to promulgate ordinances under Article 239B. |
| **28th Amendment** | 1972 | Repealed Article 314 and inserted **Article 312A** to abolish special conditions of service and privileges of erstwhile Indian Civil Service (ICS) officers. |
| **29th Amendment** | 1972 | Added Kerala land reform amendment acts to the Ninth Schedule. |
| **30th Amendment** | 1972 | Removed the monetary threshold of ₹20,000 for civil appeals to the Supreme Court under Article 133, basing appeals solely on substantial questions of law of general importance. |
| **31st Amendment** | 1973 | Increased the maximum elective strength of the **Lok Sabha from 525 to 545 members** (states increased from 500 to 525; UTs reduced from 25 to 20). |
| **32nd Amendment** | 1973 | Inserted **Articles 371D and 371E** to provide equitable opportunities in public employment and education for different regions of Andhra Pradesh (Six-Point Formula). |
| **33rd Amendment** | 1974 | Amended Articles 101 and 190; empowered Speaker/Chairman to verify that resignations of MPs/MLAs are voluntary and genuine before accepting them. |
| **34th Amendment** | 1974 | Added 20 State land ceiling and tenure reform acts to the Ninth Schedule. |
| **35th Amendment** | 1974 | Terminated the protectorate status of **Sikkim** and conferred the status of an **"Associate State"** of the Indian Union; added the Tenth Schedule (original). |
| **36th Amendment** | 1975 | Made **Sikkim a full-fledged 22nd State** of the Indian Union; inserted Article 371F; repealed the Tenth Schedule of 1974. |
| **37th Amendment** | 1975 | Formed a Legislative Assembly and Council of Ministers for the Union Territory of **Arunachal Pradesh**. |
| **38th Amendment** | 1975 | Declared the satisfaction of the President/Governor in declaring Emergencies and issuing ordinances as final, non-justiciable, and beyond judicial review. |
| **39th Amendment** | 1975 | Placed the election of the President, Vice-President, Prime Minister, and Speaker beyond the scrutiny of courts; added dozens of Central laws to Ninth Schedule (partially struck down in *Indira Gandhi v. Raj Narain*). |
| **40th Amendment** | 1976 | Substituted Article 297 to vest all minerals, natural resources, and continental shelf/exclusive economic zone (EEZ) wealth within territorial waters in the Union; added 64 acts to Ninth Schedule. |
| **41st Amendment** | 1976 | Raised the **retirement age of State Public Service Commission (SPSC)** and Joint PSC members from **60 to 62 years** (UPSC remained at 65). |
| **42nd Amendment** | 1976 | **"Mini-Constitution"**: Added words **"Socialist, Secular, and Integrity"** to Preamble; inserted Part IVA (**Fundamental Duties - Art 51A**) and Part XIVA (**Tribunals - Arts 323A/323B**); shifted five subjects from State List to Concurrent List (Education, Forests, Weights & Measures, Protection of Wild Animals/Birds, Administration of Justice); made Cabinet advice binding on President; froze delimitation of seats till 2001; curtailed judicial review powers. |
| **43rd Amendment** | 1977 | Restored the jurisdiction of the Supreme Court and High Courts regarding judicial review and writ issuance; repealed Article 31D (anti-national activities law enacted in 42nd Amendment). |
| **44th Amendment** | 1978 | Enacted by the Janata Government to undo Emergency excesses: Replaced "internal disturbance" with **"armed rebellion"** for National Emergency; mandated written Cabinet advice for declaring Emergency; **removed Right to Property from Fundamental Rights** (relegated to a legal right under **Article 300A**); barred suspension of Articles 20 and 21 during Emergency; restored 5-year tenure of Lok Sabha and Assemblies; provided constitutional protection to media reporting of parliamentary proceedings (Article 361A). |
| **45th Amendment** | 1980 | Extended reservation of seats for SCs/STs and nomination of Anglo-Indians in Lok Sabha and Assemblies for another 10 years (until 1990). |
| **46th Amendment** | 1982 | Enabled State governments to levy sales tax on consignment sales and transactions involving "works contracts" and hire-purchase. |
| **47th Amendment** | 1984 | Added 14 more State land reform enactments to the Ninth Schedule. |
| **48th Amendment** | 1984 | Amended Article 356 to permit extension of President's Rule in Punjab beyond one year up to two years without satisfying standard conditions. |
| **49th Amendment** | 1984 | Granted constitutional sanctity and **Sixth Schedule** status to the Autonomous District Councils in the State of **Tripura**. |
| **50th Amendment** | 1984 | Substituted Article 33 to empower Parliament to restrict Fundamental Rights of intelligence agency personnel, counter-intelligence units, and telecommunication systems personnel set up for armed forces. |
| **51st Amendment** | 1984 | Reserved seats for STs in the Lok Sabha from Meghalaya, Nagaland, Arunachal Pradesh, and Mizoram, and in the Assemblies of Nagaland and Meghalaya. |
| **52nd Amendment** | 1985 | **Anti-Defection Law**: Added the **Tenth Schedule** and amended Articles 101, 102, 190, and 191; disqualified MPs and MLAs on grounds of defection from their political party. |
| **53rd Amendment** | 1986 | Conferred statehood on **Mizoram** and inserted **Article 371G** granting special constitutional guarantees to Mizo religious, social, and customary practices. |
| **54th Amendment** | 1986 | Increased the salaries of Supreme Court and High Court judges; enabled Parliament to alter judicial salaries by ordinary legislation without requiring constitutional amendment. |
| **55th Amendment** | 1986 | Conferred statehood on **Arunachal Pradesh** and inserted **Article 371H** giving the Governor special responsibility regarding law and order. |
| **56th Amendment** | 1987 | Conferred statehood on the Union Territory of **Goa**; fixed minimum assembly strength at 30 members (inserted Article 371-I). |
| **57th Amendment** | 1987 | Reserved seats for Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assemblies of the north-eastern states of Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Mizoram, and Nagaland. |
| **58th Amendment** | 1987 | Inserted **Article 394A** in Part XXII; provided for an **authoritative Hindi text of the Constitution** having the same legal sanctity as the English version. |
| **59th Amendment** | 1988 | Amended Article 356 to extend President's Rule in Punjab up to three years; allowed declaration of Emergency in Punjab on grounds of "internal disturbance" (later repealed). |
| **60th Amendment** | 1988 | Amended Article 276; raised the professional tax ceiling levied by municipalities/local authorities on individuals from ₹250 to **₹2,500 per annum**. |
| **61st Amendment** | 1989 | Amended Article 326 to **reduce the voting age from 21 to 18 years** for Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assembly elections. |
| **62nd Amendment** | 1989 | Extended reservation of seats for SCs/STs and Anglo-Indian nominations in Lok Sabha and Assemblies for an additional 10 years (until 2000). |
| **63rd Amendment** | 1989 | Repealed the controversial provisions introduced by the 59th Amendment relating to emergency powers in Punjab. |
| **64th Amendment** | 1990 | Amended Article 356 to permit extension of President's Rule in the State of Punjab for a total of three years and six months. |
| **65th Amendment** | 1990 | Replaced the single-member Special Officer for SCs/STs with a multi-member constitutional body: the **National Commission for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes** under Article 338. |
| **66th Amendment** | 1990 | Added 55 State land reform and land ceiling acts to the Ninth Schedule. |
| **67th Amendment** | 1990 | Amended Article 356 to further extend President's Rule in Punjab to a total period of four years. |
| **68th Amendment** | 1991 | Amended Article 356 to further extend President's Rule in Punjab up to a maximum period of five years. |
| **69th Amendment** | 1991 | Granted special status to Union Territory of Delhi; redesignated it as the **National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi**; created a 70-member Legislative Assembly and 7-member Council of Ministers (inserted **Articles 239AA and 239AB**). |
| **70th Amendment** | 1992 | Included the elected members of the Legislative Assemblies of **NCT of Delhi and Puducherry** in the Electoral College for Presidential elections under Article 54. |
| **71st Amendment** | 1992 | Added three languages—**Konkani, Manipuri, and Nepali**—to the Eighth Schedule (taking total recognized languages to 18). |
| **72nd Amendment** | 1992 | Provided for reservation of seats for Scheduled Tribes in the Tripura State Legislative Assembly until the adjustment of seats under Article 170. |
| **73rd Amendment** | 1992 | **Panchayati Raj Act**: Granted constitutional status to rural local bodies; inserted **Part IX (Articles 243 to 243-O)** and the **Eleventh Schedule** containing 29 functional items; instituted mandatory three-tier Panchayats, State Election Commission, and State Finance Commission. |
| **74th Amendment** | 1992 | **Nagarpalika Act**: Granted constitutional status to urban local bodies; inserted **Part IXA (Articles 243P to 243ZG)** and the **Twelfth Schedule** containing 18 functional items; provided for Nagar Panchayats, Municipal Councils, and Municipal Corporations. |
| **75th Amendment** | 1994 | Provided for the establishment of State-level Rent Tribunals to adjudicate landlord-tenant disputes and exclude jurisdiction of civil courts (Article 323B). |
| **76th Amendment** | 1994 | Placed the **Tamil Nadu Backward Classes Act, 1994 (providing 69% reservation)** in the Ninth Schedule to shield it from judicial scrutiny under the 50% ceiling rule. |
| **77th Amendment** | 1995 | Inserted **Article 16(4A)** enabling the State to provide **reservation in promotions** in public employment for SCs and STs (countered the Supreme Court's *Indra Sawhney* bar). |
| **78th Amendment** | 1995 | Added 27 State land reform laws to the Ninth Schedule (bringing total entries to 284). |
| **79th Amendment** | 1999 | Extended reservation of seats for SCs/STs and nomination of Anglo-Indians in Lok Sabha and Assemblies for another 10 years (until 2010). |
| **80th Amendment** | 2000 | Implemented Tenth Finance Commission recommendations; introduced **Alternative Scheme of Devolution**; pooled central taxes and shared them on a consolidated percentage basis with states (amended Articles 269, 270, and 272). |
| **81st Amendment** | 2000 | Inserted **Article 16(4B)** to introduce the **"Carry Forward Rule"** for unfilled reserved vacancies; stipulated that backlog vacancies do not count against the 50% reservation ceiling in subsequent recruitment years. |
| **82nd Amendment** | 2000 | Added a proviso to Article 335; empowered the State to relax qualifying marks or lower evaluation standards in examinations for reservation in promotion for SCs and STs. |
| **83rd Amendment** | 2000 | Exempted the State of **Arunachal Pradesh** from the requirement of providing reservations for Scheduled Castes in Panchayats (as the state is entirely tribal with no native SC population; amended Article 243M). |
| **84th Amendment** | 2001 | Extended the freeze on the total number of seats in the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies for another 25 years (**until 2026**); allowed intra-state territorial constituency boundary readjustment based on the 1991 Census. |
| **85th Amendment** | 2001 | Amended Article 16(4A) with retrospective effect (from June 1995) to grant **"consequential seniority"** to SC and ST government servants in matters of promotion. |
| **86th Amendment** | 2002 | **Right to Education**: Inserted **Article 21A** making free and compulsory education for children aged 6–14 a Fundamental Right; substituted Article 45 (early childhood care up to 6 years); added Fundamental Duty under **Article 51A(k)** for parents/guardians. |
| **87th Amendment** | 2003 | Amended Articles 81, 82, 170, and 330 to mandate that internal delimitation and rationalization of constituencies be based on the **2001 Census** figures instead of the 1991 Census. |
| **88th Amendment** | 2003 | Inserted Article 268A and Entry 92C in Union List to provide constitutional framework for **taxes on services** (Service Tax levied by Union and collected/appropriated by Union and States). |
| **89th Amendment** | 2003 | Bifurcated the combined National Commission; established two separate constitutional bodies: the **National Commission for Scheduled Castes (NCSC)** under **Article 338** and the **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes (NCST)** under newly inserted **Article 338A**. |
| **90th Amendment** | 2003 | Maintained the existing representation of Scheduled Tribes and non-Scheduled Tribes in the Assam Legislative Assembly from the **Bodoland Territorial Areas District (BTAD)** under Article 332(6). |
| **91st Amendment** | 2003 | **Capped Council of Ministers & strengthened Anti-Defection**: Limited size of Council of Ministers (including PM/CM) to **15% of the total strength** of Lok Sabha / State Assembly (minimum 12 for States, Art 75/164); removed the split-defence (1/3rd split exemption) in Tenth Schedule; mandated disqualification of defectors from ministerial posts. |
| **92nd Amendment** | 2003 | Added 4 languages—**Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, and Santhali**—to the **Eighth Schedule**, raising the total count of constitutionally recognized languages to **22**. |
| **93rd Amendment** | 2005 | Inserted **Article 15(5)** enabling the State to provide reservations for socially and educationally backward classes (OBCs), SCs, and STs in **admissions to educational institutions**, including private aided and unaided institutions (except minority institutions). |
| **94th Amendment** | 2006 | Relieved Bihar from the constitutional obligation of having a dedicated Minister for Tribal Welfare and extended this mandatory provision to newly created states: **Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh**, alongside Odisha and MP (Article 164(1)). |
| **95th Amendment** | 2009 | Extended reservation of seats for SCs/STs and nomination of Anglo-Indians in Lok Sabha and Assemblies for another 10 years (from 60 to 70 years, i.e., **until 2020**; amended Article 334). |
| **96th Amendment** | 2011 | Substituted the word **"Odia"** for "Oriya" in the Eighth Schedule and renamed the state name entry in the First Schedule. |
| **97th Amendment** | 2011 | **Co-operative Societies**: Made right to form cooperatives a Fundamental Right under **Article 19(1)(c)**; inserted DPSP under **Article 43B**; added **Part IXB (Articles 243ZH to 243ZT)** governing cooperative structures *(Part IXB was later partially struck down by the Supreme Court in 2021 for lacking state ratification, surviving only for multi-state cooperatives)*. |
| **98th Amendment** | 2012 | Inserted **Article 371J** to provide special affirmative provisions for the backward **Hyderabad-Karnataka region** (now Kalyana-Karnataka) including regional boards, quota in state public employment and education. |
| **99th Amendment** | 2014 | Established the **National Judicial Appointments Commission (NJAC)** to replace the Supreme Court Collegium system for judicial appointments (inserted Articles 124A, 124B, 124C). *(Struck down as unconstitutional by the Supreme Court in 2015 as violative of Judicial Independence / Basic Structure).* |
| **100th Amendment** | 2015 | Operationalized the **1974 Land Boundary Agreement (LBA)** with **Bangladesh**; exchanged 111 Indian enclaves in Bangladesh for 51 Bangladeshi enclaves in India; altered boundaries of Assam, West Bengal, Meghalaya, and Tripura in the First Schedule. |
| **101st Amendment** | 2016 | **Goods and Services Tax (GST)**: Transformed India's indirect tax regime; inserted **Article 246A** (concurrent taxing power for Centre and States), **Article 269A** (IGST on inter-state supply), and **Article 279A** (established the **GST Council** headed by the Union Finance Minister). |
| **102nd Amendment** | 2018 | Granted constitutional status to the **National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC)** under **Article 338B**; inserted **Article 342A** empowering the President to specify the Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC) list. |
| **103rd Amendment** | 2019 | Provided up to **10% reservation for Economically Weaker Sections (EWS)** in higher education and initial appointments in public employment among citizens not covered by SC/ST/OBC quotas (inserted **Articles 15(6) and 16(6)**). |
| **104th Amendment** | 2020 | Extended reservation of seats for SCs and STs in the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies for another 10 years (**until January 25, 2030**); **discontinued the nomination of Anglo-Indians** to Lok Sabha and State Assemblies (amended Article 334). |
| **105th Amendment** | 2021 | Clarified and restored the power of **State Governments and Union Territories to maintain and identify their own State lists of Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBCs)**; amended Article 342A to nullify the Supreme Court's interpretation in the Maratha reservation case (*Jaishri Laxmanrao Patil*). |
| **106th Amendment** | 2023 | **Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam (Women's Reservation Act)**: Reserves **one-third (33%) of all seats for women** in the Lok Sabha, State Legislative Assemblies, and the Legislative Assembly of NCT of Delhi; inserted **Articles 330A, 332A, and 334A**; features internal one-third reservation for SC/ST women; valid for an initial sunset period of 15 years, to take operational effect following post-census delimitation. |
