> Bar Council of India (BCI)
1. Advocates Act, 1961, on the recommendations of the All India Bar Committee.
2. objective: safeguarding the rights, interests and privileges of advocates throughout India.
3. Fun:
   1. Prescribes standards of professional conduct and etiquette; exercises disciplinary jurisdiction over the bar.
   2. Sets standards for legal education; grants recognition to universities whose law degrees qualify graduates for enrolment as an advocate.
   3. Welfare Funds welfare schemes for economically weaker and physically handicapped advocates. (कल्याण) Examination (परीक्षा)
   4. Conducts the All India Bar Examination (AIBE) to grant a 'Certificate of Practice' to advocates practising law in India.
   5. Foreign Qualification Recognition (विदेशी योग्यता मान्यता) Recognises, on a reciprocal basis, foreign law qualifications obtained outside India for admission an advocate.
3. Membership
  1. comprises members elected from each State Bar Council,
  2. Attorney General of India and 
  3. the Solicitor General of India
4. Term: 5 years
5. Chairman: 
   1. the Council choose its own Chairman and Vice-Chairman from among its members, and they serve for two years.
