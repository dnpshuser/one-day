### Hybrid Popcorn
- MSP - yes (recommended by Commission for Agricultural Cost and Prices)
- 3.19 million tonne / ha - India's productivity
- global avg: 5.5-5.6 mt/ha - US/China leading producers
- production: 55 million tonne: 2025-26
- States: MP(largest) then Kar, Mh, Bihar, Telangana
- Collaborative development: ICAR-IIMR and Gourmet Popcornica
- Seeds variety used: Krishna459 and Godari234
- Mechanism of popping (फ्टने की क्रियाविधि): a hard, moisture-resistant hull surrounds a dense pocket of starch and moisture, On heating, the moisture becomes steam (भाप), building pressure until the hull bursts and the kernel turns inside out (उलटा).
- Poaceae(Grass family) -> Maize[sci.name: Zea Mays](cereal) -> popcorn(flint corn variety)
