## AI

> Issue: Requires large vol of water for cooling
1. Google's planned 1-GW hyperscale data centre in Visakhapatnam will use air-cooling instead of water-intensive methods
2. TCS's HyperVault plans a 1-GW Al campus in Hyderabad using Direct-to-Chip (DTC) liquid cooling.

### Challenges
1. Capacity grew from ~520 MW (2020) -> 1.5-1.57 GW (2026).
2. Ministry of Power projects Al and data centres may add 26.3 GW of demand by 2031-32.
3. Power-to-heat 1:1
4. Over half of capacity in Mumbai/Navi Mumbai; hubs at Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Delhi-NCR, Jamnagar.
5. Thermal Wall: Air cooling capacity 40 kW per rack - cloud racks: 5-10 kW; modern Al racks: 120-150 kW.
6. Urban Heat Island

### Cooling Tech
1. Direct to Chip
2. Immersion cooling
3. Evaporative cooling: water absorbs heat and evaporates - water intensive

### Alternative
1. Geothermal heat dumping
2. using AI heat in renewable energy - to run turbines
