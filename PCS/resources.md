Based on the video, here is the comprehensive booklist and resource guide recommended for the 70th BPSC Prelims, categorized by subject:

* **History (0:42):** Focus on **Ghatna Chakra** for all previous year questions (PYQs) and related conceptual clarity. It is recommended over *Spectrum* due to time constraints.
* **Geography (1:27):** Study two **Class 11 NCERT books** (Physical Geography and Indian Geography). For **mapping**, watch the 'Places in News' video series from the *IAS PCS Factory* YouTube channel (2:10).
* **Polity (2:30):** Use **Ghatna Chakra** to cover PYQs and related concepts. *Laxmikanth* is not recommended for this short timeframe.
* **Science (3:15):** Utilize **Class 8, 9, and 10 NCERTs** along with the science section of **Lucent**. For Science & Technology current affairs, refer to select chapters from **PT 365** (focusing on ICT, Biotechnology, Nanotechnology, and space missions).
* **Bihar Special (4:07):** Read *Anisha Bharti's* **Bihar Samagra** (KBC Nano publication). Both objective and subjective versions are useful, with a preference for the objective version for MCQs.
* **Economy (4:39):** Cover **Class 11 NCERT (Indian Economy)** and **Class 12 NCERT (Macroeconomics)**. Additionally, refer to the **Economic Survey and Budget** (both for India and Bihar) via YouTube summaries.
* **Current Affairs (5:22):**
    * **General:** Use **SP-DI** for quick revision and **Pariksha Manthan (Lakshya 1, 2, 3)** for detailed news. For revision, watch the **6-month current affairs video** by *Gaurav Sir* (Utkarsh Classes).
    * **Bihar Specific:** Use **SP-DI (Bihar special edition)** or **Eduteria** for initial pages regarding summarized state news, economy, and polity. Follow specific updates on election performance and ministerial representation.
