### 1. Milankovitch Orbital Cycles & Anthropocene Climate Forcing

1. **Astronomical Pacemaker:** Pleistocene glacial-interglacial cycles are governed by three cyclical orbital perturbations:
* **Eccentricity (~100 kyr):** Orbit shifts from circular to elliptical ($e = 0.005 \leftrightarrow 0.058$), varying annual insolation by $\sim0.1\%$.
* **Obliquity (~41 kyr):** Axial tilt oscillates between $22.1^\circ$ and $24.5^\circ$ (currently $23.44^\circ$), controlling high-latitude seasonal temperature severity.
* **Precession (~21–26 kyr):** Gyroscopic axial wobble alters perihelion/aphelion alignment relative to solstices.


2. **Thermodynamic Threshold:** Glaciation is triggered when low obliquity and aphelion summers prevent winter snowpack from melting at $65^\circ\text{N}$.

4. **Anthropocene Rupture:** Greenhouse radiative forcing ($>425\text{ ppm }\text{CO}_2$) operates at $\sim0.2^\circ\text{C}$ per decade, overriding natural orbital cooling trends by two orders of magnitude.
