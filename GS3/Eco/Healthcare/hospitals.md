2. Public hospital perception

3. Committee Report
   - Committee Report: The Parliamentary Standing Committee on Health (176th Report) brings this tension into sharp focus.
     - 368 recommendations
     - 5 Major recommendations:
       - Cross subsidization for poor patients and PMJAY benefetiaries
       - cost estimates to avoid billing shocks
       - Standardised package rates
       - greater transparency in hospital billings
       - regulating room tarrifs
   - Concerns on overregulations
     - discourage FDI/FPI
       - sol: distinguish between productive investment vs harmful concentration
       - productive investment: builds new hospitals, adopt new tech, lower prices, accept low income patients
       - harmful concentration: massive corporate chains, monopolize the market, kill off competition
4. Schemes
   - PM-JAY: world's largest public healthcare scheme
     - Sep 13, 2018 
     - 5L Health Insurrance per family annually
     - coverage: 12 crore vulnerable families - pib
     - aim: affordable, quality and accessable
     - covers: Primary, Secondary and Tertiary HC
5. Way forward
   - Regulate Total Episode cost instead of individual services
   - Diagnosis based packages instead of charges for individual testing costs
   - Strenghten public healthcare which should be a credible alternative instead of last resort
   - Reform PM-JAY to include quality checks instead of measuring volumes
