### Blue Led
1. Intro
2. What is it ?
3. Application
4. Impact in everyday's life
5. Concerns
6. Conclusion

1. Intro
	- The blue LED solved the missing link in efficient white light, transforming semiconductor physics into an everyday technology with global energy and social benefits.
2. What is it ?
	1. A blue LED is a semiconductor device that emits blue light when electrons and holes recombine across a suitable band gap.
	2. Efficient blue emission required gallium nitride (GaN), a wide-band-gap semiconductor, and practical methods to grow high-quality crystals and create p-type layers.
	3. The breakthrough was recognised by the 2014 Nobel Prize in Physics awarded to Isamu Akasaki, Hiroshi Amano and Shuji Nakamura. **Source: Nobel Prize, 2014.**
3. Application
	1. White LEDs: blue light excites a phosphor to produce broad-spectrum white light, or combines with other LEDs.
	2. Displays, projectors, traffic signals, vehicle lamps and smartphone backlighting.
	3. Blu-ray optical storage, high-density data transmission and specialised ultraviolet/blue-light sources.
	4. Water purification and sterilisation research through short-wavelength light.
4. Impact in everyday's life
	1. LED bulbs provide more light per unit of electricity and last longer than incandescent lamps.
	2. Lower electricity demand reduces household expenditure, peak load and power-sector emissions.
	3. Compact, robust lighting expands off-grid solar lighting, street lighting and digital displays.
	4. The technology enabled bright, energy-efficient screens and modern solid-state lighting.
5. Concerns
	1. Poor-quality LEDs may have glare, flicker and blue-light exposure concerns affecting comfort and sleep patterns.
	2. Semiconductor manufacturing and discarded electronic products create resource and e-waste burdens.
	3. Efficiency, colour quality and disposal require standards, repairability and recycling.
6. Conclusion
	- Combining efficient blue emitters with responsible manufacturing, safer lighting standards and circular electronics can extend the innovation's benefits while reducing its environmental footprint.
