### Tech soln: highway toll collection
1. Intro
2. what tech involved
3. advantages
4. challenges & limitations
5. potential hazards
6. proposed solutions
7. Conclusion

1. Intro
	- India's toll collection has evolved from cash booths to interoperable digital mobility, making highways faster, more transparent and data-enabled.
2. what tech involved
	1. **FASTag:** RFID tag linked to a prepaid or bank account is read by a scanner at the toll plaza and the fee is deducted electronically.
	2. **Automatic Number Plate Recognition:** Cameras use optical character recognition to identify vehicles and support barrier-free tolling.
	3. **GPS and satellite tolling:** GNSS can calculate distance travelled and charge users without fixed plazas, subject to reliable positioning and privacy safeguards.
	4. **Central platforms:** National Electronic Toll Collection integrates banks, issuers, acquirers, plazas and dispute resolution.
3. advantages
	1. Less waiting, fuel consumption, cash handling and emissions at plazas.
	2. Better revenue assurance, audit trails and highway-usage data.
	3. Interoperability improves user convenience across states and corridors.
	4. Supports logistics efficiency and contactless public services.
4. challenges & limitations
	1. Tag damage, insufficient balance, network failure and scanner errors cause disputes.
	2. Older vehicles, rural users and commercial fleets may face onboarding difficulties.
	3. ANPR struggles with poor lighting, damaged plates and privacy-sensitive data handling.
	4. GNSS tolling requires accurate maps, cybersecurity, billing interoperability and protection against spoofing.
5. potential hazards
	1. Leakage of vehicle-location and payment data can enable surveillance or profiling.
	2. Cyberattacks, identity theft, cloned tags and fraudulent number plates can disrupt revenue collection.
	3. Automated penalties may wrongly burden users without accessible grievance redressal.
6. proposed solutions
	1. Provide multilingual onboarding, offline fallback, transparent statements and time-bound dispute resolution.
	2. Encrypt data, minimise retention, audit vendors and follow the Digital Personal Data Protection framework.
	3. Combine FASTag, ANPR and GNSS gradually through open standards and independent security testing.
	4. Use interoperable payment systems, accessible exemptions and regular audits of algorithmic accuracy.
7. Conclusion
	- A secure, privacy-preserving and interoperable toll ecosystem can turn digital collection into smoother mobility without sacrificing citizen trust.
