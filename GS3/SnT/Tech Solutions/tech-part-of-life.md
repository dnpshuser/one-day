### Tech: part of life
1. Intro
2. key areas where tech became a part of life
3. Merits
4. Demerits
5. Conclusion

1. Intro
	- Technology has moved from being a separate tool to becoming the invisible infrastructure through which people learn, work, transact, travel and access the state.
2. key areas where tech became a part of life
	1. **Communication:** Smartphones, social media, video calls and instant translation.
	2. **Governance:** Aadhaar-enabled services, DigiLocker, online certificates, direct benefit transfer and digital grievance systems.
	3. **Economy:** UPI, e-commerce, digital banking, platform work and remote collaboration.
	4. **Health:** Telemedicine, electronic records, wearables and AI-assisted diagnosis.
	5. **Education:** Digital classrooms, open courses, simulations and personalised learning.
	6. **Mobility and homes:** GPS, FASTag, smart appliances, electric vehicles and smart-city systems.
3. Merits
	1. Convenience, speed, transparency and lower transaction costs.
	2. Wider access to education, healthcare, finance and government services.
	3. New employment, entrepreneurship, innovation and disaster-response capabilities.
	4. Data-driven decisions and greater inclusion when designed for accessibility.
4. Demerits
	1. Digital exclusion due to device, connectivity, language and disability barriers.
	2. Privacy breaches, cybercrime, surveillance and misuse of personal data.
	3. Misinformation, screen dependence, mental-health effects and social polarisation.
	4. Job displacement, algorithmic bias, e-waste and energy consumption of digital infrastructure.
5. Conclusion
	- A rights-based digital society, combining innovation with privacy, accessibility, human oversight and green design, can ensure technology enlarges human capability rather than human vulnerability.
