## Intro
"One Nation, One Election" (ONOE) proposes synchronizing the electoral cycles of the Lok Sabha and all State Legislative Assemblies so they are held simultaneously.

* **Handling Premature Dissolution:** In the event of a hung parliament/assembly or a successful no-confidence motion, fresh elections will be held, but the new House will serve *only for the remainder (unexpired term)* of the original 5-year cycle, ensuring synchronization is not broken.
* **Constitutional Amendments:** Implementing ONOE requires amending 
  1. A-83 - Duration of House of Parliament, 
  2. A-85 - Session of Parliament, prorogation and dissolution, 
  3. A-172- Duration of State Legislature, 
  4. A-174- Session of State Legislature, prorogation and dissolution, 
  5. A-356- Clauses of Presidential Rule.
* **State Ratification:** Amendments regarding local bodies (Article 324A) and the single electoral roll (Article 325) will require ratification by at least 50% of the states as they alter the federal structure.

## Procedure
1. Synchronization of LS and SLA: Presidents notification of Appointed Date(1st Sitting of new LS): SLA formed after this date will have truncated tenures to expire alongside LS 5 year term.
2. Synchronization of Local body elections within 100 days of national/state elections
3. Single electoral roll under A-325
4. Handling Premature Dissolution: New House will serve only for the remainder of original 5 year cycle.
5. Constitutional Amendments and State Ratification: A-83,85,172,174 and 356

## Evolution
1. 1951-67: Golden Era of ONOE
2. Cycle Break: 68,69 - SLA dissolution, 70 - Indira Gandhi's govt dissolution
3. Law Commission 170th report: 1999: Recommended reverting to simultaneous election to curm expenditure and administration paralysis.
4. In 2015, Parliamentary Standing Committee on Personal, Public Grievance, Law and Justice advocated for ONOE
5. Mar, 2024: Ram Nath Kovind Committee Report elaborating procedure for implementation of ONOE
6. Sept, 2024: Union Cabinet Approval
7. Dec, 2024: Bill introduction(129th Amendment Bill) and JPC formation


## Benefits/Arguments in Favor
1. **The Ram Nath Kovind High-Level Committee (2024):**: asynchronous elections fuel inflation, slow down economic growth, and upset social harmony.
2. **Economic Saving: NITI Aayog Discussion Paper**: ONOE can save 10KCr to exchequer and massive savings of Pol Parties curbing influence black money
3. **Mitigating Policy Paralysis: Parliamentary Standing Committee(79th Report)**: long standing administrative paralysis during MCC.
4. Coutinuous Elections - Policy uncertainity; ONOE - invester's confidence
5. **Governance over Campaigning:**
6. **Social Cohesion** limiting societal polarization

## Challenges / Arguments Against
1. Anti-Democratic truncation of newly elected government just to meet 5 year cycle
2. Undermines Federalism: Voters tend to vote the same pary in simultaneous elections unfairly benefiting National Parties subsuming regional issues.
3. Accountability risk due to guarenteed 5 year window - chances of Arrogance and unresponsiveness.
4. Security and Logistical Nightmare for ECI: Estimated 30 lakh EVM and VVPAT with CAPF deployed over entire nations
5. UK tried implementing ONOE(Fixed Term Parliament Act, 2011) but repealed due to political realities.

## Global Comparison
1. South Africa - National and Provincial Elections are held simultaneously; Provincial Elections held 2yr later
2. Sweden: Holds National, Provincial and Local Elections on the exact same day i.e. 2nd Sunday of Sept every 4 year
3. Germany: Constructive Vote of No-Confidence
