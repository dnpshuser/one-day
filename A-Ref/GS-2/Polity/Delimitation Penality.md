# Penality to southern states for successfully implementing population control in Delimitation Commission

## Intro
* Article 81 of the Constitution mandates that the ratio between a state's population and its number of Lok Sabha seats should remain uniform across India.

## Evolution
1. the 42nd Amendment (1976) froze the allocation of Lok Sabha seats based on the 1971 Census until 2000. 
2. The 84th Amendment (2001) extended this freeze until the first census published after 2026 (likely the upcoming, delayed 2021 census).
3. 1971 onwards
   1. NS: UP/Bihar/Raj -> significant pop growth
   2. SS: Tn/Ker/Kar/Ap -> replacement level fertility rate
4. The "Penalty for Success": SS: 129->112, NS 120->143
5. **Federal Imbalance:**: Inadequate representation of developed southern states
6. Violated: "One Person, One Vote, One Value": Rajasthan MP represents 30L people vs Kerala MP rep 18L people
7. Fiscal Angle: Centre mandated 15FC to use 2011 Census instead of 1971 vehmently opposed by Southern States
8. Article 329(a): Delimitation Commission can't be challenged in any court

## Indices
1. TFR: High (UP: 2.35, Bihar: 2.98)	Low/Below Replacement (TN: 1.4, Kerala: 1.5)
2. NITI Aayog Health Index: Bihar-lowest, TN: top ranker
3. Per Capita Income(NSO): Bihar-lowest, Tn: top ranker

## Dilemma
1. *Path A (Strict Population Basis):* A-81: Constitutional mandate of uniform population wise seat allocation
2. *Path B (Continue Freeze):* Violation of ONE PERSON ONE VOTE

## Diagram
1. **Final Diag**: TN,Kel,AP,Kar: 129 -> 112, UP,Bihar: 120->143 

## SS Achievements
1. TFR ~1.5
2. GDP contribution high TN~9.5%
3. Healthcare facilities improved
4. Industrial setup TN, Services - BLR, HYD

## Challenges/Issues
1. **Fiscal loss:** FC increasingly use new census data, reducing the South's tax share.
2. "Penalty for Success" creates a deep North-South political and fiscal divide.
3. Higher tax share without adequate representation of SS
