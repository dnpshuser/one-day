---

1. Intro
Recurring seasonal floods destroy crops, damage homes, and disrupt lives, turning natural monsoon rainfall into an annual socio-economic catastrophe across India

2. Causes
   1. Anthropogenic causes
      1. floodplain encroachment
      2. wetland loss
      3. deforestation
      4. urbanisation
      5. poor drainage
   2. Natural causes
      1. heavy rainfall
      2. cyclonic storms
      3. glacial lake outburst floods
      4. flash floods
      5. river floods
      6. siltation of rivers
      7. land subsidence

3. how to convert to
  1. sustainable source of irrigation
     - store: reservoirs, check dams, recharge structures.
     - Community-managed ponds and floodplain irrigation
  2. all-weather inland navigation
     - redirect to Seasonal rivers
     - use dredging and channel deepening

4. Mitigation measures
   - Floodplain zoning 
   - restrict settlement in high-risk areas.
   - Upstream watershed management, 
   - afforestation,  
   - wetland restoration.
   - Improved forecasting, 
   - early warning sys, 
   - emergency response.
   - Embankments, spillways, and reservoir regulation must be complemented with ecological and community measures.

5. Conclusion
Transforming reactive relief into proactive flood resilience demands empowering local urban bodies and enacting river basin laws, as urged by the 2nd ARC and Mihir Shah Committee.
