### Troposphere
1. Intro
2. Significance in weather and climate
3. Conclusion

---

1. Intro: The troposphere, the lowest atmospheric layer containing 75–80% of its mass, is the wettest layer and hosts nearly all clouds and weather phonomena.
    
2. significance in weather and climate
   1. Thermodynamics
      1. equitorial heating of sea drives ocean currents
      2. Green house effect: traps terrestrial radiation maintening earth's heat
      3. Normal Lapse Rate
      4. Temp inversion
   2. Macro-climatic effects
      1. Ocean Atmosphere telecommunication
         1. El Nino, La Nina, IOD, MJO, Monsoon
      2. Jet Streams
      3. Air Mass
   3. Hydrological cycles
      1. All kinds of Precipitation
         1. rainfall, hailstrom, snowfall
      2. Aerosol and Particulate matter forming hygroscopic nuclei
      3. Ocean currents with wind directions
   4. Biosphere support

3. Conclusion: Climate change alters tropospheric weather patterns and intensifies extremes like heatwaves, cyclones and tsumanis, demanding urgent intervention under SDG 13 (Climate Action).
