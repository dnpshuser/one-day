### Troposphere
1. Intro
2. Significance in weather and climate
3. Conclusion

---

1. Intro: The troposphere, the lowest atmospheric layer containing 75–80% of its mass, is the wettest layer and hosts nearly all clouds and weather phonomena.
    
2. significance in weather and climate
    - **Weather phenomena:** All weather events (clouds, rain, storms) occur here.
    - **Greenhouse effect:** Contains greenhouse gases that keep the Earth warm.
    - **Normal Lapse Rate:** Temperature decreases with altitude, driving convection.
    - **Convection:** Vertical movement of air, crucial for weather systems.
    - **Water vapor concentration:** Holds almost all of the atmosphere's water vapor.
    - **Jet streams:** High-speed air currents influencing weather patterns are located at its upper boundary (Tropopause).
    - **Pollutant trap:** Traps pollutants near the surface.

3. Conclusion: Climate change alters tropospheric weather patterns and intensifies extremes like heatwaves, cyclones and tsumanis, demanding urgent intervention under SDG 13 (Climate Action).
