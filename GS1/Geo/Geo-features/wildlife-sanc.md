### wildlife sanctuaries
1. Intro
Wildlife sanctuaries are protected areas established to conserve biodiversity, support ecosystem functions, and safeguard species in their natural habitats. In India, they are critical for balancing development and ecological preservation.

2. Significance
- They protect endangered species, migratory birds, and unique habitats.
- Sanctuaries help maintain ecological balance, seed dispersal, pollination, and food chains.
- They support local livelihoods through eco-tourism, forest produce, and community-based conservation.
- They also serve as climate resilience zones, preserving species and habitats under changing environmental conditions.
- Source: Wildlife Protection Act, 1972; National Wildlife Database; MoEFCC.

3. Conclusion
Wildlife sanctuaries represent both environmental ethics and practical conservation strategy, ensuring that biodiversity remains a foundation for sustainable development. Source: Wildlife Protection Act, 1972; MoEFCC.
