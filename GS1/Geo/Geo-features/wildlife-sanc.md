### wildlife sanctuaries
1. Intro
2. Significance of wildlife sanctuaries
3. Conclusion

---

1. Intro: Wildlife sanctuaries are protected areas established to conserve biodiversity, support ecosystem functions, and safeguard species in their natural habitats. 

2. Significance
   - protect endangered species, migratory birds, and unique habitats.
   - maintain ecological balance, seed dispersal, pollination, and food chains.
   - local livelihood: eco-tourism, forest produce, and community-based conservation.
   - climate resilience zones, preserving species and habitats under changing environmental conditions.

3. Conclusion: Wildlife sanctuaries represent both environmental ethics and practical conservation strategy, ensuring that biodiversity remains a foundation for sustainable development.
