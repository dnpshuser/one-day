2. soil distribution in India
	1. **Alluvial:** Indo-Gangetic and coastal plains; rice, wheat, sugarcane, jute and pulses.
	2. **Black:** Deccan plateau; clayey, moisture-retentive and suited to cotton, soybean and pulses.
	3. **Red and yellow:** Peninsular uplands; millets, pulses, groundnut and oilseeds with irrigation and balanced inputs.
	4. **Laterite:** High-rainfall plateaus; tea, coffee, cashew and plantation crops after soil management.
	5. **Arid and mountain soils:** Millets, pulses, livestock, horticulture and forests, but high erosion risk.
3. cropping pattern on soil types
	1. Soil, rainfall, irrigation and markets together determine crop choice.
	2. Rice-wheat dominates irrigated alluvial plains; cotton-soybean and pulses are important in black-soil regions.
	3. Millets and pulses suit drier soils, while plantations and horticulture suit laterite and hill soils.
4. challenges of soil degradation
	1. Water and wind erosion, ravines, desertification and topsoil loss.
	2. Salinity, alkalinity and waterlogging from poor irrigation and drainage.
	3. Declining organic carbon, micronutrient imbalance, compaction and excessive chemical inputs.
	4. Industrial contamination, urban sealing and climate extremes.
5. Measures to adopt
	1. Soil-test-based fertilisation, compost, green manure, biofertilisers and crop-residue management.
	2. Crop rotation, mixed cropping, cover crops, conservation tillage and agroforestry.
	3. Contour bunds, check dams, shelterbelts, watershed development and micro-irrigation.
	4. Reclaim saline soils through drainage, gypsum where suitable and salt-tolerant crops.
6. Govt schemes to improve soil health
	1. Soil Health Card Scheme: nutrient assessment and fertiliser recommendations. **Source: Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.**
	2. Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana: irrigation expansion and water-use efficiency.
	3. National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture and Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana: climate-resilient and ecological farming.
7. Conclusion
	- Anchoring land restoration through regenerative agriculture and soil carbon enrichment is vital to combating desertification and securing SDG 15 (Life on Land).
