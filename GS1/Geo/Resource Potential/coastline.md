---

1. Intro
	- India's coastline is an ecological shield, economic gateway and resource zone whose sustainable use can advance blue growth without sacrificing coastal resilience.
2. what resources are found along the coastline
	1. **Marine living resources:** 
   	1. Fisheries, 
   	2. aquaculture, 
   	3. seaweed, 
   	4. mangroves and 
   	5. coastal biodiversity.
	2. **Minerals and energy:** 
   	1. Beach-sand minerals, 
   	2. offshore oil and gas, 
   	3. salt, 
   	4. tidal and offshore wind energy.
	3. **Economic resources:** 
   	1. Ports, 
   	2. shipping, 
   	3. shipbuilding, 
   	4. tourism, 
   	5. coastal industries and 
   	6. marine biotechnology.
	4. **Ecosystem services:** 
   	1. Mangroves, 
   	2. coral reefs, 
   	3. dunes, 
   	4. wetlands and 
   	5. seagrasses 
   	6. provide carbon storage, 
   	7. nursery grounds and 
   	8. storm protection.
3. coastline resource potential of India in sustainable development
	1. India's coastline is about 7,500 km including mainland and islands. **Source: Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Maritime India Vision 2030.**
	2. Promote sustainable fisheries, cold chains, mariculture and value addition rather than uncontrolled extraction.
	3. Develop green ports, coastal shipping, offshore wind, tidal research and blue-carbon projects.
	4. Use integrated coastal-zone planning to balance livelihoods, conservation, tourism, ports and urbanisation.
4. Status of hazard preparedness
	1. Cyclone forecasting, early-warning dissemination, evacuation and disaster-resilient shelters have improved.
	2. The Indian Tsunami Early Warning Centre provides Indian Ocean alerts. **Source: INCOIS, Ministry of Earth Sciences.**
	3. Gaps remain in last-mile connectivity, local capacity, erosion mapping, nature-based protection and resilient infrastructure.
5. ongoing projects along the coastline in different sectors
	1. Sagarmala: port modernisation, connectivity, coastal shipping and port-led development. **Source: Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways.**
	2. National Centre for Sustainable Coastal Management: coastal-zone mapping and science-based management.
	3. Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: fisheries infrastructure, aquaculture and fisher welfare. **Source: Department of Fisheries.**
	4. National Coastal Mission and MISHTI: mangrove conservation and restoration. **Source: MoEFCC.**
6. Conclusion
	- Harnessing coastal potential requires integrating Sagarmala-led port modernization with Integrated Coastal Zone Management (ICZM), balancing maritime prosperity with ecological resilience.
