1. Intro
	- As the world's most populous nation, India's demographic scale presents an unprecedented dividend—poised to drive global economic growth and productivity.
2. Gangetic population distribution
	1. Causes
   	1. Fertile alluvial plains, 
   	2. perennial rivers, 
   	3. level terrain and long agricultural history support dense settlement from Punjab and Haryana through Uttar Pradesh, Bihar and West Bengal.
   	4. Irrigation, 
   	5. transport corridors and cities reinforce density, 
	2. Challenges
   	1. floods, 
   	2. land fragmentation and 
   	3. rural underemployment create pressure.
3. Total fertility rate
	1. India's total fertility rate is 2.0 children per woman, below the replacement level of 2.1. **Source: NFHS-5, 2019-21, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.**
	2. The transition is uneven: southern states face ageing concerns, while parts of central and eastern India need stronger reproductive healthcare, education and employment.
4. population projection
	1. Ageing - World Population Prospects 2024
	2. productive jobs for youth
	3. preparing social security and healthcare for an ageing population.
5. how to reap demographic dividend ?
	1. Ensure quality school education, 
	2. foundational literacy, 
	3. digital access and 
	4. employable skills.
	5. Raise female labour-force participation through childcare, safe transport, flexible work and equal wages.
	6. Shift from low-productivity agriculture to 
   	1. manufacturing, 
   	2. care economy, 
   	3. tourism, 
   	4. logistics and 
   	5. modern services.
	7. Improve 
   	1. nutrition, 
   	2. primary healthcare, 
   	3. reproductive choice and 
   	4. migration-friendly urban planning.
6. on going schemes to enhance population employability
	1. Skill India Mission, 
	2. Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana and 
	3. National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme.
	4. National Education Policy 2020: multidisciplinary, vocational and outcome-based learning.
	5. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana and VB-G RAM G support rural skills and livelihood security.
	6. MUDRA, 
	7. Startup India, 
	8. Stand-Up India and 
	9. Digital India support entrepreneurship and formalisation.
7. Conclusion
	- Harnessing the demographic dividend demands aggressive investments in quality education, skilling, and healthcare, converting sheer numbers into sustained human capital.
