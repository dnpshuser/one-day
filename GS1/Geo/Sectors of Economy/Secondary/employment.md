### Resource based stratedy for promoting employment - India
1. Intro
2. Benefits
3. Challenges
4. Conclusion
 
1. Intro
	- India's employment challenge is to convert a large labour force into productive, formal and dignified work through regionally balanced economic growth.
2. Benefits
	1. Labour-intensive manufacturing, food processing, construction, tourism and care services can absorb semi-skilled workers.
	2. Local enterprises reduce distress migration and connect primary producers with value chains.
	3. Formal jobs expand social security, tax capacity, productivity and household demand.
	4. Resource-based clusters enable value addition near farms, forests, mines and fisheries.
3. Challenges
	1. Skill mismatch, informality, low female participation, regional inequality and inadequate non-farm jobs.
	2. Automation can displace routine work; micro-enterprises face credit, technology and market constraints.
	3. Job quality, occupational safety, social security and reliable employment data remain concerns.
	4. **Source:** Periodic Labour Force Survey 2023-24, MoSPI, for current labour-market estimates.
4. Conclusion
	- A skills-to-jobs ecosystem built on MSMEs, green industries and gender inclusion can fulfil Article 41, SDG 8 and India's Viksit Bharat ambition.
