1. Intro
	- The Blue Revolution refers to the rapid growth and sustainable management of worldwide aquaculture and fisheries production.
2. concept
	1. sustainable development of 
   	1. inland fisheries, 
   	2. marine fisheries, 
   	3. aquaculture, 
   	4. mariculture, 
   	5. processing and cold-chain infrastructure.
	2. It aims to raise fish production, fisher incomes, employment and protein availability without exhausting aquatic ecosystems.
3. Evolution
	1. Traditional capture fisheries expanded into mechanised marine fishing and freshwater aquaculture.
	2. The focus has shifted towards diversification, value addition, biosecurity, traceability and fisher welfare.
4. Status
	1. India is among the world's leading fish producers and the second-largest aquaculture producer. **Source: FAO, The State of World Fisheries and Aquaculture 2024.**
	2. Fisheries support millions of fishers, fish farmers, processors, traders and allied workers. **Source: Department of Fisheries, Annual Report 2023-24.**
5. Challenges
	1. Overfishing, 
	2. juvenile catch, 
	3. habitat destruction, 
	4. pollution and climate-induced changes in fish stocks.
	5. High post-harvest losses, 
	6. weak cold chains, 
	7. cyclones, 
	8. indebtedness and unsafe working conditions.
	9. Disease outbreaks, 
	10. feed costs, 
	11. coastal conflicts and inadequate access to formal credit.
6. Solutions
	1. Science-based catch limits, 
	2. seasonal bans, 
	3. mangrove restoration and co-management with fishing communities.
	4. Modern harbours, 
	5. cold chains, 
	6. hygienic processing, 
	7. insurance, 
	8. skill training and traceable exports.
	9. Promote integrated multi-trophic aquaculture, native species and climate-resilient practices.
7. Govt initiatives
	1. Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: infrastructure, aquaculture, value chains and fisher welfare.
	2. Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund, Blue Revolution scheme and Kisan Credit Cards for fishers. **Source: Department of Fisheries.**
8. Conclusion
	- Scaling the Blue Revolution requires integrating mariculture technologies with sustainable fisheries management, anchoring inclusive coastal prosperity within the mandate of SDG 14 (Life Below Water).
