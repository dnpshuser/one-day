### Q. Why India is a subcontinent
### A. 
1. Intro: 
   - A subcontinent is an extensive, distinct landmass physically detached from a continent. India earns this status through distinct plate tectonics, physical enclosure, and climatic unity
2. Reasons: 
      1. Tectonic origin: India from Gondawana super continent vs Asia from Laurasian supcont
      2. Physiographic isolation: Hindu Kush, Himalayas, Arkan Yoma, Indian Ocean
      3. Climate: Tropical monsoon
      4. Distinct biodiversity with unique flora and fauna - 3/8 global biodiversity hotspot in Indian subcontinent
      5. ongoing tectonic activity along Himalayas
      6. Socio Cultural and Civilizational distinctiveness.
3. Conclusion: From Gondwanan tectonic origin to shared monsoonal rhythms, the Indian landmass constitutes a self-contained physiographic and civilizational subcontinent within Asia
