3. importance
   1. strenghtens exchange rates
   2. soft power diplomacy - pol and corporate rep help shape favourable policies and trade agreements

   4. Asset diversification: Bank deposits, gold, real estate -> AIFs/ReITS/Invits - directly funding infra, renewable energy and manufacturing

4. Challenges
   1. Semi-skilled and Low skilled workers in gulf countries: job insecurity due to volatile oil prices
   2. Restrictive immigration and Visa Policy: H-1B Visa with 100K dollars fees
   3. Brain Drain: loss of top talent for better ecosystem
   4. Generational Disconnect: 2nd and 3rd Gen of OCI and PIO
   5. Limited political participation in India
   6. Philanthropic hurdles with strict regulations under FCRA-2010
   7. Data and policy fragmentation: e-Migrate and MADAD exists but data scattered
   8. Racism and Xenophobia
   9. Asymmetric Remittence Dependence
   10. Cost of Remittance (विप्रेषण की लागत): the global average cost through traditional banking channels remains above the SDG 10.c target of 3%.
   11. Informal Channels (अनौपचारिक माध्यम): hawala (हवाला) persists, bypassing KYC and formal forex channels and creating a risk of money laundering (धन शोधन).
   12. Illegal Recruitment and Human Trafficking with false job promise
5. Engagement initiatives
   1. OCI: lifelong multiple entry visa
   2. Pravasi Bharatiya Divas: biennial celebrations on 9th Jan with Pravasi Bharatiya Samman Award
   3. VAJRA (Visiting Advanced Joint Research) Scheme: enables overseas Indian scientists (प्रवासी भारतीय वैज्ञानिक) to conduct high-end collaborative research in Indian public-funded institutions
   4. PRABHASS (Pravasi Bharatiya Academic and Scientific Sampark): connects the global Indian S&T community (वैश्विक भारतीय विज्ञान एवं प्रौद्योगिकी समुदाय) with Indian ministries to solve domestic technological.
6. Initiatives to boost remittence
   1. RBI's FCNR(B) to swap USD/INR instantly and free of charges
   2. UPI internationalization: UPI-PayNow(Singapore) allows instant person to person remittence
   3. India-UAE local currency settlement reducing conversion cost
7. steps to leverage better
   1. Globalizing India's stack of UPI-RuPay to adv economies reducing intermediary charges
   2. Reviving Diaspora bonds
   3. Institutionalize Brain circulation
   4. Sector specific diaspora advisory council
   5. Diaspora for Economic Diplomacy (आर्थिक कूटनीति हेतु प्रवासी): the Indian diaspora in the US played vital role in lobbying for the Indo-US Civil Nuclear Agreement, finalised in 2008.
   6. Strengthening Blue-Collar Protections with social security agreements
   7. Revamping the OCI Framework bringing diaspora-led-venture funds.
8. Conclusion
   1. The diaspora is no longer only a remittance channel (विप्रेषण माध्यम) — it is a supplier of capital, technology, entrepreneurship and diplomatic leverage.
   2. The diaspora is no longer merely a demographic dividend outside India’s borders
(सीमाओंके बाहर का जनसांख्यिकीय लाभांश मात्र नहीं) — it is now a critical instrument of capital formation
