4. Impacts
   - Health impacts: heatstroke, dehydration, cardiovascular and respiratory issues, increased mortality rates.
   - Economic impacts: reduced labor productivity, increased energy demand, damage to infrastructure, agricultural losses.
   - Environmental impacts: wildfires, loss of biodiversity, water scarcity, soil degradation.
5. Way forward
   - Implement early warning systems and heat action plans.
   - Promote public awareness campaigns on heatwave preparedness and safety measures.
   - Enhance urban planning and infrastructure to reduce heat exposure (e.g., green spaces, reflective surfaces).
   - Strengthen healthcare systems to manage heat-related illnesses.
   - Encourage research on climate-resilient crops and sustainable agricultural practices.
