### The Public Examination (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024
1. Intro
The Public Examination (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024 reflects a decisive governance response to cheating, corruption, and institutional trust erosion in high-stakes recruitment and examinations.

2. Aims and objectives
- Prevent leakage of question papers, impersonation, and collusive malpractice in public examinations.
- Strengthen integrity in recruitment and academic evaluation systems.
- Protect the rights of honest candidates and preserve public faith in merit-based opportunities.
- Improve accountability among institutions, intermediaries, and service providers involved in exam conduct.
- Align exam governance with democratic values of fairness, equality, and administrative legitimacy.

3. Important clauses and coverage
- Covers organised cheating, paper leakages, impersonation, and misuse of technology in exam processes.
- Criminalises collusion among officials, candidates, and middlemen involved in unfair conduct.
- Provides for stricter penalties and legal action against orchestrated malpractice networks.
- Empowers enforcement agencies to investigate and prosecute examination-related corruption.
- Enhances preventive governance by focusing on institutional integrity, surveillance, and compliance.
- Source: The Public Examination (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024; Ministry of Education; official notification.

4. Conclusion
The Act is a step toward accountable governance and constitutional fairness, reinforcing meritocracy, public trust, and the state’s duty to ensure equitable opportunity in recruitment and education.
