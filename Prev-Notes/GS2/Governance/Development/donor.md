### Donor Agency
1. Intro
Donor agencies play a significant role in financing development and social programmes, but their involvement must not displace local ownership or community accountability.

2. Increasing dependency concerns
- Heavy dependence on external funding can distort local priorities and reduce policy autonomy.
- Donor-linked programmes may focus on external goals rather than community needs.
- Sustainability often becomes fragile once external funds decline.

3. Effect on community participation
- External funding can reduce local initiative if communities see programmes as externally driven.
- It may weaken community ownership and lead to a project-centric rather than people-centric approach.
- Development becomes more effective when beneficiaries participate in design, monitoring, and governance.

4. Potential to make inclusive growth
- Donor agencies can support marginalised communities, public goods, and innovative social programmes.
- They can bring technical expertise, accountability mechanisms, and cross-learning.
- With proper governance, they can strengthen inclusion, women’s empowerment, and sustainability.

5. Conclusion
Donor support can be valuable for development, but democratic accountability and local participation must remain central to achieving inclusive and sustainable growth.
