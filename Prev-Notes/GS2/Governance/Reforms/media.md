### New Media: Increase in FDI
1. Intro
Foreign Direct Investment in new media reflects the intersection of digital growth, media liberalisation, and governance in a globalised information economy.

2. Pros
- Brings capital, technology, and global expertise to the media sector.
- Expands access to digital content, innovation, and broadcasting capacity.
- Promotes competition, productivity, and media pluralism.
- Supports new formats, online journalism, and digital ecosystems.

3. Cons
- Concentration of media ownership may threaten plurality and democratic debate.
- Foreign influence raises concerns about editorial independence and national security.
- Profit motives may compromise public-interest journalism and rural accessibility.
- Digital markets can amplify misinformation, surveillance, and algorithmic manipulation.

4. Conclusion
FDI in new media can accelerate innovation and access, but public interest, editorial independence, and media pluralism must remain central to policy and regulation.
