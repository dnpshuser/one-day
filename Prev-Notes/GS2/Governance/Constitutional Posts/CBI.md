#### CBI
1. Intro
The Central Bureau of Investigation is the premier investigative agency of the Union, entrusted with crime investigations, anti-corruption work, and support to the rule of law.

2. Procedure and role of State and Centre
- The CBI functions under the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions, while retaining operational independence.
- It investigates cases referred by the Centre, state governments, or courts in specific circumstances.
- In state matters, CBI requires prior consent under the federal structure unless directed by the Supreme Court.
- Its role includes investigating corruption, economic offences, and serious crimes with national implications.
- Coordination with state police remains essential, but central oversight may raise federalism concerns.
- The agency is expected to balance efficiency with constitutional federal balance and procedural fairness.
- Source: Delhi Special Police Establishment Act; Supreme Court judgments on CBI autonomy.

3. Conclusion
The CBI is crucial for maintaining institutional integrity, yet its effectiveness depends on autonomy, accountability, and constitutional sensitivity to federalism and due process.
