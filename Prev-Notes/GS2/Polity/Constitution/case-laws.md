1. Intro
Judicial decisions have continuously expanded the meaning of the Constitution, translating abstract rights into practical constitutional guarantees. 

2. Gender justice
   - Articles 14, 15, 16, 21, and 39 support gender equality.
   - Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan (1997) created a legal framework against workplace sexual harassment.
   - Navtej Singh Johar (2018) recognised autonomy and dignity in matters of sexuality.
   - These judgments deepen constitutional morality and substantive equality.

3. Environmental justice
   - MC Mehta cases linked environmental degradation to Article 21 and the right to life.
   - The judiciary has interpreted public health, pollution control, and ecological balance as constitutional concerns.
   - This has shaped environmental governance and sustainable development jurisprudence.
   - Source: MC Mehta judgments; environmental law cases.

4. Judicial review and constitutional supremacy
   - I.R. Coelho reaffirmed judicial review as part of the Basic Structure.
   - Judicial review prevents legislation from ignoring constitutional values.
   - It ensures accountability of the executive and legislature.
   - It is central to preserving federalism, rights, and democratic balance.

5. Conclusion
The judiciary acts as constitutional interpreter and guardian, advancing dignity, inclusion, and accountable governance in line with SDG 16.
