### Political Parties
1. Party structure and democratic representation
2. National parties and centralisation
3. State parties and local autonomy
4. Evolution of party system in India
5. Leadership, accountability and reforms
6. Conclusion

---

1. Intro
Political parties are the vehicles of democratic representation, translating public aspirations into governance and accountability. 

2. Centralization - National Party
   1. Reasons and eg
   - National parties often have centralised leadership, strong organisational networks, and wide-scale electoral reach.
   - Examples include Congress, BJP, and other pan-India formations.

3. Autonomy - State party
   1. Reasons and eg
   - Regional parties emphasise local identity, caste, language, and state-specific issues.
   - Their autonomy reflects India’s federal diversity and social plurality.

4. Evolution and Phase transition: Congress -> BJP
- The party system has evolved from dominance by a single national party to competitive multi-party federalism.
- The shift reflects social mobilisation, coalition politics, and changing voter expectations.

5. Conclusion
A robust multiparty democracy is essential for inclusive governance, representation, and constitutional responsiveness in a plural society.
