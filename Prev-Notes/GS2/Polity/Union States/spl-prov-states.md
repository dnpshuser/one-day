### Special provisions to some States

3. Jammu and Kashmir and other cases
4. Administrative and political implications

---

1. Intro
Special provisions under Part XXI acknowledge India’s territorial, cultural, and historical diversity, allowing tailored governance in specific regions. 

2. Jammu and Kashmir (2016)
   1. Extent of Temporary provisions under A-370.
   - Article 370 was a temporary constitutional arrangement reflecting the region’s special status.
   - Its abrogation in 2016 marked a historic constitutional shift with major political implications.
   2. Future prospects
   - The focus now is on integration, governance efficiency, and social justice while preserving constitutional legitimacy.

3. Nagas
   1. MoPNG directives threat to Nagas exceptional status - Art-371A
   - Article 371A protects Naga customary law and land ownership, but it also requires balancing local identity with national integration.

4. Expanded based on current affairs to other states of Art-371_.
- Special provisions exist for states like Assam, Manipur, Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh, and Sikkim, reflecting strategic and cultural priorities.

5. Conclusion
Special provisions are necessary for India’s plural constitutional order, provided they respect national unity, equality, and constitutional morality.
