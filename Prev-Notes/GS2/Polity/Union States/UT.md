1. Intro
Union Territories are directly administered by the Union, reflecting the constitutional principle that some regions require special central oversight. 

2. Jammu and Kashmir Legislative Assembly
   1. Nature
   - It is a legislature with powers defined by special constitutional arrangements and the Union’s supervisory role.
   2. Power
   - It exercises legislative and executive functions within the scope defined by constitutional law.
   3. Functions
   - It legislates on state-like subjects within the limited sphere granted by the Union framework.

3. conflicts: MLA and Lieutenant Governor - administration of Delhi
   1. Evolution - 69th CAA
   - Delhi’s governance has been shaped by constitutional ambiguity between legislative authority and executive control.
   - Conflicts between the elected Assembly and the Lieutenant Governor reflect the tension between democratic representation and central oversight.

4. Conclusion
Union Territories require a careful constitutional balance between democratic representation and effective governance, preserving federal equilibrium and accountability.
