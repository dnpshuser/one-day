### Presiding Officers
1. Procedural neutrality and institutional authority
2. Speaker and legislative order
3. Impartiality and fairness
4. Vice President as Rajya Sabha Chair
5. Constitutional conventions and legitimacy
6. Conclusion

---

1. Intro
The Speaker and the Chairman are constitutional custodians of parliamentary procedure, ensuring order, fairness, and legitimacy in legislative debate. 

2. Speaker
   1. Role in maintaining Law and Order
   - The Speaker regulates proceedings, maintains discipline, and ensures orderly debate.
   2. Impartiality
   - Neutrality is crucial to uphold democratic fairness and prevent executive capture.
   1. 'Once a Speaker, Always a Speaker' 
      - The convention of neutrality and detachment after leaving office reflects the dignity of the office.
      1. adoption scope
      - It should be understood as a constitutional norm, not as a political shield.
      1. implication
      - It strengthens institutional trust and public confidence.

3. Vice President
   1. Role as Chairman of Rajya Sabha
   - The Vice President presides over the Rajya Sabha, maintaining procedure and rules in a non-partisan manner.

4. Conclusion
Strong presiding officers are essential for democratic deliberation, institutional balance, and the constitutional value of accountable representation.
