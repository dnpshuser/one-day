### Parliamentary Privileges
1. Legislative independence and accountability
2. Meaning and scope of privileges
3. Benefits and democratic rationale
4. Risks of misuse and limits
5. Codification and reform options
6. Conclusion

---

1. Intro
Parliamentary privileges safeguard the independence of elected representatives and the dignity of the legislature, ensuring effective deliberation and accountability. 

2. enumerate privileges
- Freedom of speech in Parliament, freedom from arrest in certain cases, right to regulate internal proceedings, and exclusive rights over proceedings and records.

3. Benefits
- These privileges ensure lawmakers can debate without fear or executive intimidation.
- They protect legislative autonomy and democratic representation.

4. Concerns
- Misuse of privilege can undermine accountability and free expression.
- It is essential to prevent privileging impunity over scrutiny.

5. reasons for the non-codification
- Historical evolution, parliamentary conventions, and the need for flexibility in legislative independence.
- However, codification could improve clarity and reduce ambiguity.

6. actionable solutions/measures
- Codify privileges under broad legal principles while preserving democratic independence.
- Encourage ethical standards, transparency, and internal discipline.

7. Conclusion
Privileges are essential for parliamentary freedom, but they must be used responsibly to protect democratic accountability and constitutional morality.
