### Parliamentary Committees
1. Legislative scrutiny and accountability
2. Role, structure and functions
3. Estimates and PAC oversight
4. Standing and department committees
5. Impact on governance quality
6. Conclusion

---

1. Intro
Parliamentary committees are the workhorses of legislative scrutiny, enabling detailed examination of laws, finances, and governance beyond the plenary stage. 

2. Role, Structure, Functions and impact of each committee with eg
   1. Estimates Committee
   - Reviews budget estimates and suggests efficiency measures.
   2. Public Accounts Committee
   - Examines government expenditure and audit reports to ensure accountability.
   3. Standing Committees
   - Provide continuous scrutiny of legislation and policy.
   4. Ad Hoc Committees
   - Investigate specific matters as and when needed.
   5. Financial Committees
   - Scrutinise budgetary allocations and public finance discipline.
   6. Departmentally Related Standing Committees
   - Examine ministry-wise policy and legislation in depth.

3. Conclusion
These committees strengthen parliamentary accountability, improve legislative quality, and deepen institutional trust in democratic governance.
