### Micro Watershed Development Projects
1. Intro
2. Concepts
3. Conclusion

---


1. Intro
Micro-watershed development projects are local community initiatives that manage and conserve soil, water, and vegetation within small land units ranging from 100 to 1,000 hectares

1. Concepts
- What is it ?: A micro watershed is a small drainage unit that can be managed through local planning and community participation.
- Interventions 
  - contour bunding, 
  - check dams, 
  - gully plugs, 
  - farm ponds, 
  - vegetation restoration, 
  - recharge structures.
- obj: to conserve soil moisture, reduce runoff, improve groundwater recharge, and sustain rural livelihoods.
- Community-based governance and participatory planning are central to success.


1. examples and cities
   - Mh, Kar, AndP, Raj - drought prone regions
   - tribal and rural regions - linked with MGNAREGA

2. Effects and impacts
   - Improved groundwater recharge  
   - better soil moisture 
   - support agriculture and horticulture.
   - Reduced 
     - soil erosion, 
     - siltation, and 
     - flash runoff during heavy rainfall.
   - Increased resilience against drought and climate variability
   - Development of common property resources and local ecological benefits.

3. Government initiatives/projects
   - Integrated Watershed Management Programme (IWMP) 
   - Convergence with MGNREGA, 
   - PMKSY, 
   - state-level water conservation programmes.

4. Conclusion
Institutionalizing community-led watershed development under PMKSY is essential for decentralized aquifer recharge, soil conservation, and doubling rural farm incomes.
