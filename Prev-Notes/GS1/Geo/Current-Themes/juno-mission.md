### Juno Mission of NASA
1. Intro
The Juno mission, launched by NASA in 2011, is a landmark exploration programme designed to study Jupiter, the largest planet in the Solar System. It has transformed our understanding of planetary formation, magnetism, and the early history of giant planets.

2. mission
- Juno entered Jupiter’s orbit in 2016  
- studying the planet’s atmosphere, magnetic field, gravity field, and internal structure.
- to ans: how giant planets formed ? do they have a solid core ?
- analyzing Jupiter’s auroras, radiation belts, and atmospheric dynamics.

1. significance/key discoveries
- atmosphere is more complex than thought, 
- powerful storms and deep atmospheric structures.
- confirmed: non-uniform interior + magnetosphere is highly dynamic.
- imprv understanding: how Giant planets formed
- extraordinary auroral activity due to intense radiation and magnetif feild.

1. conclusion
Juno is a high-value planetary science mission because it connects solar system evolution, atmospheric physics, and magnetospheric science in one comprehensive exploration programme. Source: NASA/JPL.
