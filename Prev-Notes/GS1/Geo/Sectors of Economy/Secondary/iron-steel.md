### Iron and Steel Industry
1. Intro
2. Reasons for
   1. Distribution away from raw material - India
   2. Distribution away from raw material - Global
3. Conclusion
 
---

1. Intro
   - Serving as the foundational backbone of heavy engineering and infrastructure, the iron and steel industry drives capital formation and macroeconomic modernization.
2. Reasons for
   1. Distribution away from raw material - India
      1. Coastal and market-oriented plants benefit from imported coking coal, ports, large markets and modern logistics.
      2. Secondary steel and 
      3. electric-arc-furnace units locate near scrap, electricity and urban demand.
      4. Odisha-Jharkhand-Chhattisgarh belts retain ore-based advantage, but newer plants increasingly use multimodal supply chains.
   2. Distribution away from raw material - Global
      1. Japan, 
      2. South Korea and 
      3. parts of Europe import ore and coal through deep-water ports and export finished steel.
      4. China locates major capacity near coal, ports and huge domestic markets; 
      5. Gulf plants use gas-based direct reduction.
      6. Technology, energy cost, water, market access and environmental norms now influence location as much as ore.
3. Conclusion
   - Decarbonizing production through Green Hydrogen, scrap-based electric arc furnaces, and domestic coking-coal alternatives is pivotal to anchoring India's Net-Zero industrial transition.
