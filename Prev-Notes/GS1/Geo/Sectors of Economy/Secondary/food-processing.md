1. Intro
	- Adding value to agricultural produce through modern food processing is the most effective bridge to transform farmers into rural entrepreneurs." — Dr. M.S. Swaminathan
2. Factors for localization of Agro-based food processing industry in NorthWest India
	1. Large wheat, rice, cotton, dairy, fruit and vegetable production from Punjab, Haryana, western Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan.
	2. Assured irrigation, 
	3. mechanised farming, 
	4. cold winters for storage and 
	5. proximity to Delhi-NCR's large market.
	6. Roads, railways, warehouses, mandis, electricity, packaging supply and established agro-industrial skills.
	7. Food Corporation procurement and flour, rice, dairy, sugar and beverage clusters create backward and forward linkages.
3. Conclusion
	- Cold-chain expansion, decentralised processing and sustainable packaging can turn North-West India's farm surplus into inclusive value addition aligned with SDG 2 and 9.
