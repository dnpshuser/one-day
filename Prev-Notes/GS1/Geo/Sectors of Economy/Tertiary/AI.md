1. Intro
	- Artificial intelligence and drones are reshaping spatial planning by converting complex geographic data into faster, predictive and evidence-based decisions.
2. Application of AI and drones in locational and aerial planning
	1. **Site selection:** GIS-AI models combine slope, soil, land use, hazard, transport and population layers to identify suitable locations.
	2. **Urban planning:** Satellite imagery and drones map encroachment, traffic, buildings, heat islands, drainage and infrastructure gaps.
	3. **Agriculture:** Drones survey crop stress, irrigation, pest infestation and yield; AI converts imagery into targeted farm advisories.
	4. **Disaster management:** Rapid aerial mapping supports damage assessment, evacuation routes, flood forecasting and post-disaster reconstruction.
	5. **Environment:** AI detects deforestation, mining, coastline change and pollution; drones monitor wildlife and difficult terrain.
	6. **Infrastructure:** Digital twins and predictive analytics improve road alignment, asset maintenance, logistics and energy planning.
	7. **Safeguards:** Use high-quality local data, human verification, privacy protection, drone airspace rules and algorithmic accountability.
3. Conclusion
	- Responsible AI-enabled planning can advance SDG 9 and 11, strengthen cooperative governance and help India build resilient, inclusive and climate-smart spaces.
