### Indian Regional Navigational Satellite System (IRNSS)

2. Evolution
3. How it helps in navigation ?
4. Need/Benefits

6. Conclusion
 
---

1. Intro
	- NavIC converts India's space capability into sovereign positioning services, strengthening navigation, disaster response, logistics and strategic autonomy.
2. Evolution
	1. The Indian Regional Navigation Satellite System was developed after dependence on foreign navigation signals exposed strategic vulnerabilities.
	2. The system was renamed NavIC and designed to provide Standard Positioning Service for civilians and Restricted Service for authorised users.
	3. It uses geostationary and geosynchronous satellites, ground stations, precise clocks and user receivers.
3. How it helps in navigation ?
	1. Satellites transmit time-stamped signals; a receiver calculates its position from the time difference of signals from multiple satellites.
	2. NavIC provides coverage over India and approximately 1,500 km beyond its boundary. **Source: ISRO, NavIC overview.**
	3. It supports road, rail and maritime navigation, fleet tracking, surveying, timing, disaster warnings and location-based services.
4. Need/Benefits
	1. Reduces dependence on foreign systems and improves resilience during conflict or signal denial.
	2. Accurate timing supports telecom, banking, power grids and digital infrastructure.
	3. Enables fisheries advisories, emergency alerts, precision agriculture and transport efficiency.
5. Significance for India
	1. Strengthens national security, border management, space diplomacy and indigenous technology.
	2. Integration into smartphones, vehicles and public systems can deepen the domestic space economy.
	3. L1-band services improve mass-market compatibility and adoption.
6. Conclusion
	- Wider NavIC adoption can advance SDG 9, secure digital public infrastructure and support India's vision of technological self-reliance by 2047.
