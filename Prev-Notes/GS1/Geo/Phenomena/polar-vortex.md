### Polar vortex
1. Intro
2. Types
3. How does it occur
4. What are its impacts
5. Conclusion

---

1. Intro: The polar vortex is a large-scale, persistent, low-pressure cyclonic circulation of extremely cold air situated over Earth’s polar regions - upper tropospheric and lower stratospheric regions. While it exists year-round, it strengthens dramatically during winter in both the Northern and Southern Hemispheres.
2. Types
   1. Stratospheric PV - 15-50km
   2. Tropospheric PV enclosed by PJS - 0-10km
3. How does it occur
   1. Extremely low temp
   2. air subsides down
   3. upper atm - low Pressure
   4. surrounding winds deflects due to P gradient
   5. coriolis force: diflection towards Right
   6. circular cyclonic movement of wind - observed - called PV
   7. splits when PJS meanders
4. What are its impacts
   1. Severe cold waves
   2. Stratospheric PV prevents O3 widening O3-hole
5. Conclusion: Tracking polar vortex disruptions is vital for forecasting severe mid-latitude cold snaps, erratic Western Disturbances, and the thermodynamic impacts of Arctic amplification.
